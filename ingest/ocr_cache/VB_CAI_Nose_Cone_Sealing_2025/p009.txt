[NOTE SECTION]
NOTE:
1. ITEM-1 & 2 SHALL BE SUITABLY CUT TO MATCH WITH THE BOTTOM FRP COUPLER COVER PROFILE. IT SHALL BE FIXED WITH THE OBSTACLE DEFLECTOR WITH SCREWS OR WITH SUITABLE ADHESIVE(MMA) TO MAINTAIN AIR PROOF. A GAP OF 10MM SHALL BE MAINTAINED BETWEEN FRP COUPLER BOTTOM AND ITEM 1&2.
2. ITEMS 1 & 2, 3 & 12 AND 4 & 5 ARE MIRROR TO EACH OTHER AND IT IS MARKED AS *.
3. ITEM-1 & 2 SHALL BE OF SS 304 (ALTERNATE MATERIAL) WITH 2mm THICKNESS. ITEM-3 TO 5 & 12 SHALL BE OF SS 304 (ALTERNATE MATERIAL) WITH 1mm THICKNESS.
4. AFTER FIXING THE ITEM-1, THE GAP BETWEEN SHEET & COUPLER COVER BOTTOM SHALL BE CLOSED WITH THE RUBBER BEADING OF REPUTED MAKE SUCH AS M/SDIRAC (PART NO- 209-0221-00-00000) OR SIMILAR. SIMILARLY, AFTER FIXING ITEM-3, THE GAPS SHALL BE PROPERLY CLOSED WITH RUBBER BEADING. RUBBER BEADING SHALL BE PROPERLY FIXED WITH SEALANT SO THAT IT REMAINS IN THE POSITION DURING RUNNING CONDITIONS. INSTEAD OF RUBBER BEADING, THE SAME SHALL BE CLOSED WITH FOAM(ITEM-15) & GLUE (ITEM-16), TAPE (ITEM 17) ALSO.
5. FOR FIXING ITEMS 1 & 2, 6mm DIA DRILL HOLE SHALL BE DONE ON FRONT COVER OF OBSTACLE DEFLECTOR AT PROPER LOCATIONS SO THAT IT WILL COMPLETELY COVER THE GAP BETWEEN COUPLER COVER & OBSTACLE DEFLECTOR.
6. THERE MAY BE MINOR VARIATIONS IN THE DIMENSIONS IN THE ITEMS 1 TO 5 & 12 DUE TO VARIATIONS IN THE FRAME STRUCTURE & FRP OUTER BODY. IT MAY BE ADJUSTED DURING FITMENT IF ANY.
7. FOR FIXING ITEMS 3 & 12, 6mm DIA DRILL HOLE SHALL BE DONE ON BOTTOM PLATE OF WIPER MOUNTING BRACKET AT PROPER LOCATIONS SO THAT IT WILL COMPLETELY COVER THE GAP BETWEEN CORNER OF FRP NOSECONE COVER AND BOTTOM PLATE OF WIPER MOUNTING BRACKET. ITEMS 4&5 SHALL BE FIXED WITH SCREWS ON ITEM 3&12 RESPECTIVELY AT LOCATIONS WHICH SHALL COVER THE MAXIMUM OPENING ON THE FRONT AREA.
8. SIDE CLOSING SHEET (ITEM 13) SHALL BE USED TO CLOSE THE UPPER TRIANGULAR PORTION OF SIDE BRACKET EITHER SIDES (USED FOR MOUNTING FRP COUPLER COVER) WITH RIVETING OR PASTING WITH SUITABLE MMA OR SCREWED AND PROPER RELIEF TO BE GIVEN FOR INSERTING THE ITEM 3 & 12.
9. ITEM 14 SHALL BE FIXED AT THE BOTTOM SIDE OF WIPER MOUNTING BRACKET WITH RIVET OR SCREWING. 6mm DIA DRILL HOLE SHALL BE DONE ON THE FRONT NOSECONE BRACKET, IF SCREWS ARE USED FOR FIXING.
10. INSULATION MATERIALS SHALL BE CUT & PASTED AT LOCATIONS SHOWN AS @ AFTER CLEANING SHELL MEMBERS WITH ADHESIVE TO SPEC ICF/MD/SPEC 93 OR WITH ANY ICF APPROVED ADHESIVES ONLY.
11. FIXING OF ITEM 1&2 SHALL BE DONE AFTER THE FRP COUPLE COVER FITMENT AND FIXING OF ITEM 3 TO 5 AND 12 SHALL BE DONE AFTER FITMENT OF MARKER CUM TAIL LIGHT.
12. ITEM 15 SHALL BE INSERTED THROUGH THE GAPS BETWEEN FRP NOSECONE & SS FRAME STRUCTURE ON EITHER SIDES FROM BOTTOM AFTER PROPER APPLICATION OF ADHESIVE(ITEM-16). FINALLY THE OPENING SHALL BE CLOSED WITH ACP SHEET.ACP SHEET SHALL BE FIXED WITH RIVET. IT SHALL BE FURTHER SEALED WITH SUITABLE SEALANT OR WITH ITEM-17 IN ORDER TO AVOID WATER OR AIR-INGRESS.
13. FOR ALL NOSECONES UNDER PRODUCTION, ITEM-15 SHALL BE INSERTED (INSIDE THE DRIVER CAB)BETWEEN THE SIDE WINDOW FRAME BASE AND FRP OUTER BODY WITH ITEM-16 ADHESIVE. ALSO ITEM-15 SHALL BE PASTED ABOVE THE FRONT BRACKETS MARKED AS & WITH ITEM-16 ADHESIVE. IT SHALL BE PASTED ON CORNER PORTIONS AT SIDE PILLARS ON EITHER SIDES.
14. BEFORE THE APPLICATION OF ITEM-17, THE SURFACE TO BE CLEANED USING EMERY PAPER FOLLOWED BY 3M 94 PRIMER OR SIMILAR FOR PROPER ADHESION.
15. ITEM 17 SHALL BE OF 3M MAKE OR SIMILAR.
16. 3M FIRE BARRIER RATED FOAM FIP-1 STEP OR SIMILAR SHALL BE APPLIED WITH PROPER GUN * WITH SUFFICIENT LENGTH TO REACH THE AREA) TO CLOSE THE GAPS SHOWN AS WHICH IS INACCESSIBLE TO APPLY WITH ITEM-15.
