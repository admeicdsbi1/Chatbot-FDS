5. STEPS TO BE FOLLOWED FOR FITMENT OF COUPLER COVER

1. Top bracket of Coupler cover RH shall be sit over the channel support and to be positioned in place using locator pins. (Pl ref Fig-2 & 3)
2. Side brackets -1(B), side brackets -2(C) & one bottom centre bracket(D) are fixed with bolts loosely which in turn is aligned with bonded brackets of coupler cover RH. If alignment is ok, then tighten all the bolts using spanner. The same shall be followed for coupler cover LH. (Pl ref Fig-4)
