**Pre Requisites**
1. Lavatory door mortise handle with lock shall be fixed with back piece of SS 304.
2. Approved makes of handles shall be used (Latch Lock Article no : 209A 51200173 & Handle Article no : 51200173 Make: M/s Dormakaba ) or (Latch Lock Article no : 9228149 & Handle Article no : 9228099 Make: M/s Hettich )
3. AAB 6-3-720 or AAB 6-3-721 – 2 no's per Lavatory door.
4. M5 x 35 mm flat head counter screws & M5 hexagonal head flange nut – 4 No's each per lavatory door.
5. M/s Henkal Sealant of PU 8590 Sealant can be used along with PU 8550 Cleaner and PU 8517H Primer or equivalent of M/s Sika make.
