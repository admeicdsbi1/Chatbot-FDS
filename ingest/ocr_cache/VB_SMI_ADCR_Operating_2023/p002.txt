Instructions for DOOR operation, isolation and restricted use of ADCR Bypass switch Annexure A1/3

Things to be ensured before start of train:
1. LP/ALP should ensure that “ADCR Bypass switch” which is available in CRW panel as shown in pic should in be“AUTO”position, Similiarly Guard shall ensure the same for Rear cab.
2. Then the Guard shall open the Door overview screenpic-A in the DDU and Check whether all doors in open condition.i.e., door pictogram will be in White icons as seen in pic below.
3. If any door/s is in closed and isolated condition (Door overview will be in black as seen in pic-A below) Check the log book to verify the previous status of the isolated door/s which are already noted in the log book . If so, proceed further or make a fresh log book entry for the door.

Procedure to ensure closure of all the doors or isolate the defective doors:
1. Press the “Door warning Bell” Push Buttonin Driver Desk.Then, Door close announcement will happen.
2. Press the door close push button located in the Driver Desk To close all door
3. Now all healthy doors will start closing and DOOR summary screen will pop up in the DDU located in Driver Desk. All the doors that are closed NORMALLY will be seen as BLUE pictogram seen in pic-A below and .in drivers desk, “all door closed indication” will glow and “ Minimum one door opened” indication will extinguish
4. If the above indication not met, guard should read the the door summary screen pictogram area and act as per the table below for any particular door malfunction(refer pic-B).
