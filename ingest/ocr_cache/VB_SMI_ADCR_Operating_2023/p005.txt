Annexure B 1/3

ADCR LOGIC IN TRAINSET

A) Local Door Close Loop
Each side of every coach has its own local door close summary(left/right) relay ie., LDSRL or LRSRR, which gets energized on proving of following switches:
➢ Door closed and locked switch -( S1 ON )
➢ Emergency door operation –outside ( S3 OFF )
➢ Emergency door operation- inside ( S4 OFF )
➢ 98% door closed switch ( S8 ON )
➢ Safety relay. (SR- Normal)

B) Train level Circuit (Door Proving Logic):
The contacts of LDSRL and LRSRR of all the coaches are connected in series from one cab of the train to other cab end of the train which will be powered from DPL MCB of active driver cab through contacts of last cab activated relay.

C) All Door Closed Relay ( ADCR) circuit in DTC:
On proving of train level circuit above (point B ), Both ADCR relays will get energized from the active cab.

D) Failure of Train level Door proving logic loop:
(1) Failure of Train level Door proving logic loop is possible only in door circuit issues. In case of Door having issues in closing, it can be manually closed and isolated by authorized persons.

(2) In this condition, S5 switch will bypass all proving switches of Local door close loop ( point A) and All the indications will be discarded for this door . Passengers cannot use this door. The door can be normalised at destination by attending the issue by authorized persons

(3) If the train loop circuit is cut in any place due to failure/ door closing issue , then ADCR relay will not pickup there by the following indications will be shown in the Driver’s Desk;
➢ “Min One Door Opened” indication will glow
➢ “ All Door Closed” indication will not glow.

(4) In this situation, ADCR relay will not send command to Central Control Computer (CCC) for Traction Release. ie., Traction is disabled by CCC

E) ADCR Bypass:
On failure of Train DPL loop, Driver can bypass ADCR through ADCR bypass switch . which is available in Cab Rear Wall (CRW) Panel so that Train operation is possible without train level door proving loop for which a pop-up will display on DDU . The Loco pilot has to acknowledge and proceed for Traction.
