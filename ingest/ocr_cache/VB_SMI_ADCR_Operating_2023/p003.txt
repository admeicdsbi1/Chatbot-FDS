Working with ADCR BYPASS ON and warning
to NOTE and acknowledge:

1. After ADCR is bypassed, when the master controller is moved to traction "ADCR Feedback detected open" message will POP UP on DDU
2. LP should acknowledge the above message and bring the throttle to coast position and bring back for traction. Train will start moving if all doors are properly closed.
3. If any door is still open, then "DCU software Feedback detected open" message will POP UP in DDU, then LP should confirm the message and open the DOOR overview screen to check and ensure that all doors are either closed or isolated, if not inform technician to check and isolate the malfunctioning door before resuming further traction.

ISOLATION PROCESS

1. Operate the emergency device inside /outside i.e turn the knob to unlock position so that door will be free for manual movement,

| Internal Emergency Device(EED) | External Emergency Device(EAD) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Located in inside side coach body | Located in out side coach body |

Now shut the door with little force to close the door and then operate the isolation lever towards RED point. The lever position towards red point indicates that door in isolated position.

| Internel Isolation Device | Externel isolation Device |
| :--- | :--- |
| Located in inside door leaf | Located in outside side door leaf |
