Sub: Last shop issue size of wheels of ICF design BG main line coaches.
Ref: RDSO's letter of even No. dated 21.08.2019 & 21.07.2020.

Based on satisfactory performance feedback received from Zonal Railways, last shop issue size of 834 mm for BG coaching wheels is cleared for use on ICF design BG main line coaches on regular basis.

Zonal Railway workshops are advised to use last shop issue size of 834 mm in ICF design BG main line coaching wheels on regular basis. During tyre turning of ICF design BG main line coaching wheels to 834 mm, chamfer of 4 mm shall be kept instead of 6 mm.
