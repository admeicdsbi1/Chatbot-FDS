### Technical Values
*   **Spacer Size:** approx. 100 mm x 50 mm x 20 mm
*   **Bolt Specification:** 2x M8x30

### Component Designations
*   **Bellows:** Component to be mounted as a pre-condition.
*   **Combination bridge:** Component to be mounted as a pre-condition.
*   **Linking ceiling:** Component to be mounted as a pre-condition.
*   **Guiding bodies:** Components mounted to the wagon-interfaces.
*   **Side wall:** Component with a pin to be inserted into the guiding body.
*   **Spacer:** Mounting aid used to pre-tension the spring.

### Maintenance Procedure
1.  **Pre-condition:**
    *   The bellows is mounted
    *   The combination bridge is mounted
    *   The linking ceiling is mounted
    *   The guiding bodies are mounted to the wagon-interfaces.
2.  **Step I:** At the guiding body screw-on side (right hand side; looking orthogonal to the longitudinal axis of the wagons) pull the guiding body downwards.
3.  **Step II:** Place a spacer (Size: approx. 100 mm x 50 mm x 20 mm) in the upper opening at the guiding body – (The spacer will pre-tension the spring. It is suggested to secure the spacer with a tape to prevent it from falling into the inside of the gangway).
4.  **Step III:** Insert the pin of the side wall into the hole of the guiding body screw-on side.
5.  **Step IV:** Bolt (2x M8x30) the screw-on side of the side wall to the guiding body (screw-on side) which is already mounted to the wagon-interface
6.  **Step V:** Repeat for opposite side wall.
7.  **Step VI:** Remove the spacer out of the opening (guiding body).
8.  **Step VII:** Lock the walls
