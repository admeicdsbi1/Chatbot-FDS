Sub: Wheel Shelling - Wrong electrical connections in WSP system of LHB Coaches.

During the recent 19th CMG, it was come to notice of RDSO that in the new LHB Coaches turned out from PUs, WSP system was automatically going in Sleep Mode after 15-20 minutes of system ON and Wheel Shelling within two-three months since their commissioning in ZRs. The problem was examined in detail with OEMs and it is learnt that these coaches were found with wrong electrical connections (at the WAGO end & in Junction Boxes at coach under frame) in Wheel Slide Protection system.

In connection to this, please find enclosed the electrical wiring scheme of WSP system (with exiting & modified arrangement) of LHB brake system suppliers (for the respective OEMs brake systems of LHB Coaches) for taking following action in order to ensure 100% effective working of WSP system to tackle Wheel Shelling problem:

PUs: To ensure that all new LHB to be turned out with the aforesaid enclosed respective wiring scheme.

ZRs: Checking & ensuring wiring connections during shop schedules (SS-1, SS-2 & SS-3) of LHB Coaches on 100% basis and depots for LHB coaches having problem of WSP going in automatic sleeping mode or suffering with Wheel Shelling.

This is for kind information and necessary action please.
