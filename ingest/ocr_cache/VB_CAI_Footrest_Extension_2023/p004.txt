### Document Information
*   **Subject:** Exchange of seat cushion
*   **Document Number:** 19-017 MM
*   **Revision:** Rev. 1
*   **Date:** 03/08/2022
*   **Equipment/Model:** Train 18

### Maintenance Procedure
| Step | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Unscrew the four screws M6x16 |
| 2 | Remove cushion |
| 3 | Place the new cushion back |
| 4 | Put thread locker on the tip of the screws (Loctite 278) |
| 5 | Place the four washers DIN 125 6.4 |

### Technical Values and Specifications
*   **Screw size:** M6x16
*   **Thread locker:** Loctite 278
*   **Washer specification:** DIN 125 6.4
