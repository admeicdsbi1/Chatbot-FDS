iii. Maintenance regarding M/s Harting Type connectors used for RMPU of Vande Bharat Rakes:
a. Carbonized Connector pins to be cleaned by wiping with cotton swab dipped with Iso-Propyl Alcohol. Damaged Pins to be replaced.
Carbonised pins of M/s Harting type of connector
b. To prevent oxidation of contacts due to extended exposure of silver contacts provision of cover to be ensured.
c. Connector should not be frequently mated /unmated (connect/disconnect) until unless there is a need for RMPU rework / replacement.
d. Visual inspection to be done for proper closure of connector locking levers and check if there are any damage to connector gaskets and on additional sealing applied around the gaskets.
e. Further, M/s Harting has suggested that, wire termination shall be done with one of the below HARTING recommended tools and the same applies for removal/replacement of contacts.
