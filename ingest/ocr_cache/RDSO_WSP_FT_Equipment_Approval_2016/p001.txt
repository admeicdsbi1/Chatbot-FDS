M/s FTRTIL has offered their UIC approved AEFG2 WSP for use in LHB Coaches with Disc Brake system. During the validation trial by RDSO UIC approved AEFG2 WSP was found acceptable.

Further, Railways are requested to monitor the performance of this AEFG2 WSP till 1st SS-1 schedule and advise RDSO for taking corrective action whenever needed.
