<u>Annexure – 1</u>

**NOTE:** THE BELOW ASSEMBLY OF ADAPTER CHANNELS TO LIFT THE HEIGHT OF THE INVERTERS SHOULD BE DONE AT THE TIME OF MAINTENANCE.

<u>**Procedure to Assemble Adapter channel under the inverter units :-**</u>

* Make sure that the rake should be in discharged condition (VCB should be opened, Panto should be down) and follow the safety procedure.
* Climb onto the roof of the coach to access the RMPU unit by following all safety precautions.
* Open the Evaporator middle door by unscrewing the hardware from the RMPU unit and place aside.
* Clean the condensed water droplets on the inverter units.
* Unscrew the Inverter’s mounting hardware (M6 x 06no’s each) with Ratchet tool.
* Lift the inverter unit upwards and place the Adapter channel(**A-A675UV2-179338 , image-5**) on both sides as shown below , then fix it with same M6 x 12lg hardware with Ratchet tool.
* Then after place the Inverter units on the fixed channels using same M6 hardware ( tightening to be done using Ratchet tool).
* After fixing the Inverter’s hardware , close the top cover of the RMPU’S evaporator mid zone.
