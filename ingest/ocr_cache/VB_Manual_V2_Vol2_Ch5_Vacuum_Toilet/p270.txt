5.6.6. Replacement – Water Inlet Valve

* Disconnect water connection.
* Unscrew male elbow Ø 12 mm.

| 1 | Male Elbow Ø 12 mm | 2 | Water Inlet Valve |

* Replace water inlet valve.
* Align to squatting toilet.
* Fix it with male elbow Ø 12 mm and liquid sealing.
* Reconnect water connection.
