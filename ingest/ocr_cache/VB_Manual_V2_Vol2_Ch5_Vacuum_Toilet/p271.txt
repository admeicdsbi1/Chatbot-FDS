### Maintenance Procedures

#### Pinch Valve and Pressure Guard Module
*   **Removal:**
    1. Remove pinch valve.
    2. Unscrew pressure guard module.
*   **Installation:**
    1. Replace pressure guard module.
    2. Align to pinch valve.
    3. Install pressure guard module with liquid sealing to the refurbished or new pinch valve.
    4. Install pinch valve to squatting.

#### 5.6.8. Replacement – Vacuum Pump
*   **Procedure:** Refer to the provided diagram for the assembly orientation of the vacuum pump unit.

---

### Component Designations
*   **Pinch valve:** Component removed and re-installed during pressure guard module maintenance.
*   **Pressure guard module:** Component unscrewed from the pinch valve assembly for replacement.
*   **Vacuum Pump:** Vertical assembly unit shown in section 5.6.8.
