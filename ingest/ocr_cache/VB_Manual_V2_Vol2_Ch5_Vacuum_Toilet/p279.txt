### Maintenance Instructions
*   Replace pressure guard.
*   Adjust new pressure guard to the bracket.
*   Install male elbow and washer with liquid sealing into the pressure guard.
*   Reconnect electrical connections.

### Technical Index: Ejector Maintenance

**Document Reference:**
*   **Document Number:** IRCAMTECH/GWL/2022-23/T-18/MM/2.0
*   **Page Number:** 17 of 18

**Maintenance Procedure:**
1.  Mark electrical plug connection.
2.  Disconnect electrical plug connection.
3.  Mark pneumatic piping.
4.  Disconnect pneumatic piping.
5.  Unscrew hexagon socket head cap screw - M4 x 40 mm (2x) and detent edge washer (2x).
6.  Remove ejector.

**Technical Specifications:**
*   **Fastener Type:** Hexagon socket head cap screw
*   **Fastener Size:** M4 x 40 mm
*   **Quantity:** 2x
*   **Associated Hardware:** Detent edge washer (2x)
