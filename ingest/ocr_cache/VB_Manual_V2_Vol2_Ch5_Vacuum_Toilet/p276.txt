Maintenance Manual of VBE Trainset (V2.0) 5. Maintenance – Vacuum Bio toilet
5.6.11. Replacement – Quick Exhaust Valve

* Mark pneumatic piping.
* Disconnect pneumatic piping.
* Loosen male connectors.
* Remove the quick exhaust valve.

| 1 | Mal Elbow Ø 6 mm to Vacuum Pump | 2 | Quick Exhaust Valve |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Connector Ø 8 mm Air Outlet | 4 | Connector Ø 8 mm to Flush Water Tank |

* Replace quick exhaust valve.
* Screw in the male elbow (Pos. 1) and connector (Pos. 4) with liquid sealing to the quick exhaust valve.
* Align quick exhaust valve to water system panel.
