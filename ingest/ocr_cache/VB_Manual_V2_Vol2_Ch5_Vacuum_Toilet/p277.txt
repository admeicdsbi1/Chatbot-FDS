5.6.12. Replacement – Flush Water Tank

• Mark pneumatic piping.
• Disconnect pneumatic piping.
• Disconnect electrical connector.
• Unscrew hexagon socket cap screws M5 x 16 mm (2x) and detent edge washer (2x).
• Remove flush water tank and holder.
