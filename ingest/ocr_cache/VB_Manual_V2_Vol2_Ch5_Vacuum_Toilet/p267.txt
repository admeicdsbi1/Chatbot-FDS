### Component Designations and Descriptions
*   **Squatting toilet:** Main sanitary unit being serviced.
*   **Pinch valve:** Valve mechanism controlling waste flow.
*   **Rubber elbow:** Flexible connector between the toilet bowl and the pinch valve.
*   **Pressure guard:** Sensor/monitoring component attached to the pinch valve assembly.
*   **Pneumatic connections:** Air supply lines for valve operation.
*   **Electrical plug connection:** Wiring interface for the pressure guard.

### Maintenance Procedures

#### 5.6.4. Replacement – Pinch Valve
**Step 1: Removal of Rubber Elbow**
*   Loosen hose clamps from rubber elbow (2x).
*   Remove rubber elbow.

**Step 2: Removal of Pinch Valve**
*   Disconnect pneumatic connections.
*   Remove cap from pressure guard.
*   Disconnect electrical plug connection to pressure guard.
*   Loosen hexagon nut M4 (4x) and detent edge washers (4x).
*   Remove pinch valve from squatting toilet.
