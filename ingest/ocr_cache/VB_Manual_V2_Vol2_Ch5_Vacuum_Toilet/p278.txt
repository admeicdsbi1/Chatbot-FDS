5.6.13. Replacement – Pressure Guard

* Remove cap from pressure guard.

* Disconnect electrical plug connection to pressure guard.
* Disconnect pneumatic piping.
* Unscrew male elbow Ø 6 mm.
* Remove pressure guard.
