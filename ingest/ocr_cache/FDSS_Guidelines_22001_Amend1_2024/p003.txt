B. General requirement
1. The supplier will carry out all required work and activities with regard to design, testing and commissioning of additional functionalities for automatic fire detection cum suppression system in the Pantry car & Generator cum brake van of IR coaches.
2. Prototype approval of each supplier to ensure the compliance of the upgraded work vis-a-vis these guidelines will be carried out by RDSO. Once approved, the same will be applicable for reference of other Zonal Railways
3. If specific instructions are issued by RDSO or/and Zonal Railways for undertaking modifications to circuit design/hardware/interfaces proposed etc. to meet the Railway's requirements, the supplier will abide by these instructions.
