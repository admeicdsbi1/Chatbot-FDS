In reference to above, a team from RDSO visited WR on 25th & 26th June 2024 for route - cause identification for vibrations in coaches of Vande Bharat Express (rake ID 226430) after SS-I. On 25-06-2024, the team travelled with WR officials from MMCT to ADI and back by Vande Bharat train to experience vibrations. On 26-06-2024, the rake was thoroughly investigated and removed bogie from TC Coach No. 226439 (C-12) was disassembled and examined. It was decided that all 04 wheels of TC Coach No. 226439 (C-12) shall be sent to Lower Parel workshop, WR for further examination. Accordingly, joint inspection of wheels was conducted on 04.07.2024 at Lower Parel workshop, WR and root cause was identified. Moreover, on board data collection was done on 05.07.2024 for validation of identified root cause of vibration and corrective actions intimated to Western Railways vide letter under ref. 2. To validate the effect of corrective actions, on board data collection was again done by RDSO on 21.07.2024. Vibration issues have now been resolved in all the coaches.

In this regard, detailed report on vibrations in MMCT- GNC Vande Bharat Express/WR has been prepared and attached herewith for kind information and necessary action.
