6.5 Visible Fibres

[Image of visible fibers]

Individual fiber or bundle of fibers not covered with resin, on or above the surface.

6.5.1 Repair Procedure
a) Step-1: At identified affected areas sanding operation to be done by 320grit emery till the affected surface is removed.
b) Step-2: Apply fire retardant resin at the affected area, with proper coverage, and wait for curing.
c) Step-3: After curing, sanding operation to be done using the 320 grit & 600 grit emery
d) Step-4: After complete curing cycle is over, inspection to be carried out
