In cab display unit, the same major fault code continued to be displayed.

Trouble shooting:
1. Health status of digital input cards of MCs and TCs of BU2& BU5 were checked and found to be OK.
2. Limit switch of power couplers in BU5 between TC and MC were checked. The limit switch of MC2 (i.e. C-7) was found to be stuck. It was removed, cleaned, operated multiple times manually and re-fitted in the system. After testing, the input signal to Digital input card was found to be working normally.
3. Since there was no issue with the components associated with VCB faults, the Service Engineer went into the fault codes in DDU. On checking the fault codes, it was identified that "Input Line Contactor Stuck at close and DCLV is below min" which is indicated below.
