Sub: Standardization of 2 Amp circuit breaker in FSDS system for IR AC coaches.
Ref: (i) Railway Board's letter no. 76/M(C)/137/31 Vol. V dated 10.09.2024.
(ii) This office letter of even no. dated: 23.09.24.
(iii) This office letter of even no. dated: 12.08.24.

On subject of provision of MCB in FSDS system, Railway Board vide reference (i) advised RDSO to examine the capacity issue of MCB in 110V FSDS/FDSS circuit of LHB coaches.
In this regard, RDSO recommendations were submitted to the Railway Board via letter reference as (ii). Further, during the review meeting held on 25.09.24 and 26.09.24 at the Railway Board, the issue regarding the capacity of MCB in the 110V FSDS/FDSS circuit of LHB coaches was discussed. Based on the deliberations, Railway Board advised RDSO to re-iterate the instructions contained in letter at ref (ii) to Zonal Railways for compliance.

Accordingly, the following actions are to be followed for Standardization of 2 Amp circuit breaker in FSDS system with immediate effect:
(i) In coaches, where no MCB was fitted by the supplier during initial installation of the FSDS system in the coaches, 2 Amp MCB with limiter module has to be provided by the supplier free of cost.
(ii) In coaches, wherein circuit breaker other than of 2 Amp rating has been fitted during initial installation of the FSDS system, 2 Amp MCB with limiter module to be procured by Zonal Railways through OEMs to replace the originally fitted MCB. A budgetary rate reference from one of the suppliers is attached for your reference

Additionally, it is also requested that this work should be carried out in conjunction with the other works including segregation work for positive and negative wires associated with the FSDS system, as also advised in the RDSO letter referenced (iii).
