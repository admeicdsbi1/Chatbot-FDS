विषय: Air Gap setting between phonic wheel and WSP speed sensor
सन्दर्भ: Performance Review of LHB coaches held at Rly Board on 08.11.11 & 12.12.11.

At present, the air gap between phonic wheel and speed sensor for WSP system recommended by M/s Knorr Bremse is 0.9 to 1.4mm and by M/s Faiveley Transport is 1.0mm to 2.0mm. Railways have been facing difficulty in maintaining the gap between two different systems. The subject matter has been discussed with the OEMs to standardize the common gap between phonic wheel and speed sensor for their WSP system. M/s Faiveley Transport vide their letter No.FTRIL/Engg/RCF-WSP/2011-12 (copy enclosed) has confirmed that the gap in FTIL equipment also can be reduced to 0.9 to 1.4 mm with out any effect on the performance of the system.

In view of the above, the air gap between phonic wheel and speed sensor for WSP system is standardized as 0.9 to 1.4mm for both the system. Railways are advised to maintain air gap between phonic wheel and speed sensor accordingly.
