Instructions for provision of pre-filter for hot water boiler.

ICF Drawing No. 890-6-4-002, Alt – ‘e’

1. Item – 1 to 12, No Change
2. Item – 13 (10 inch high density spun pre-filter (5 Micron PP Spun) with housing compatible for all water purifiers size 3/8” pack of 1 of reputed make such as Eureka Forbes Aqua Guard or similar) to be procured

Objective:
To prevent scale formation in the boiler and also to ensure clear water from the boiler.

Tools required:
1. Pneumatic Drilling machine
2. Screw driver
3. Plumbing tools

Items required:
1. Suitable Reducers
2. Hose pipe
3. Teflon tape
4. Isolation cock

Steps to be followed:
1. Remove the front cover of the sink module.
2. Connect the isolation cock outlet and pre filter inlet using suitable reducers.
3. Use Teflon tapes for all joints to avoid leakage.
4. Mount the pre-filter isolation cock assembly below the sink on the two channels with fasteners supplied along with the filter.
5. Disconnect the pipe from the inlet of the hot water boiler and connect the same to the isolation cock inlet.
6. Take a new hose with reducer and connect one end to the pre filter outlet and the other end to the hot water boiler inlet.
7. Mount the front cover of the sink module.
