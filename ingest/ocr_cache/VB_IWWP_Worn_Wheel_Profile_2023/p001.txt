Sub: Use of intermediate worn wheel profile for Vande Bharat Coaches.
Ref: This office letter No. MC/WA/Genl. dated 08.08.2012.

At present, wheel profile of 29.4 mm flange thickness is being used for BG main line coaches including Vande Bharat Coaches as per RDSO drawing No. Sketch-91146 for a new wheel.

RDSO vide letter under reference has issued instructions to use intermediate worn wheel profile (RDSO drawing No. Sketch-92082) of 25 mm to 28 mm thick flange for the coaches running at maximum permissible speeds of 110 kmph and above including LHB coaches.

As the operating speed potential of Vande Bharat Coaches are same as LHB Coaches, therefore, the intermediate worn wheel profile of 25 mm to 28 mm thick flange as per RDSO drawing No. Sketch-92082 is also applicable for Vande Bharat Coaches.
