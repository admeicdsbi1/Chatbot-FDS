All Doors Closed Relay Mismatch With Software

Door open based traction will be prohibited in following cases.
1. ADCR input is HIGH(HW TL Status) and all door close status not received through software.

****************************************************************************************************
**************************************************ISOLATION PROCEDURE**************************************************
****************************************************************************************************

If the ADCR is high (That means all doors closed hardware information is
okay), and all door close software information is not okay
1. In this case a pop up will be appeared and motor man should ensure the
door close status physically, in addition to that there is a status screen
available to see the individual door open and close status.
2. After ensuring that doors are closed user should press the pop up
acknowledgement.
3. Up on the confirmation from the motor man traction is allowed.
