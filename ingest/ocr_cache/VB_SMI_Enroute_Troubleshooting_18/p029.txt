BP Low pressure Detected

********************************ISOLATION PROCEDURE********************************
************************************************************************************

1. Ensure that auto brake handle is in "Charge" position and MR is healthy in the rake (>7.5Bar)
2. If the auto brake handle is in "Charge" position, Go to the air spring screen and identify in which cab BP is low.
3. Check for any leakages in BP line, in that coach.
4. If the BP is leaking, Isolate the BP for the coach through isolation cocks and release the EP brake using isolation cocks
5. Go to BP override settings screen and do the BP override in that particular coach.
6. If no leakage is found, then directly Go to BP override settings screen and do the BP override in that particular coach.
