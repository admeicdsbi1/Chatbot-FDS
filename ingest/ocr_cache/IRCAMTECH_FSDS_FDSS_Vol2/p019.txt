### LED Indication on HOCHIKI FIRELINK-25

*   **Fire** - Illuminates when the alarm level has been reached and the appropriate time delays have expired.
*   **Fault** - Illuminates when the unit has a fault and a fault signal is being sent to the fire panel.
*   **OK** - Illuminates to show normal operation when there are no faults.
*   **OK (Flashing)** - The OK lamp will flash during the period when the detector is first learning about its environment.
