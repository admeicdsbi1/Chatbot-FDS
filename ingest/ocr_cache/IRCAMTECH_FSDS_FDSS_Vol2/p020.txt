### Inline Filter (Air Filter Box)

![Inline Filter (Air Filter Box)](image_3)

Inline Filter (air filter box) is a component used in ventilation systems to remove contamination and particulate matter from the air before it enters a specific area or equipment. It is typically installed in the air supply line to filter out dust.

*Note: In N & S SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD (Make- HOCHIKI), Inline filter is not used.*
