#### Right Panel Instructions

THIS COACH IS FITTED WITH VERY EARLY SMOKE DETECTION AND ALARM SYSTEM WITH AUTOMATIC BRAKING

* **LED INDICATION 1 (GREEN):** POWER SUPPLY TO BRAKE VALVE IS OK
* **LED INDICATION 2 (GREEN):** BRAKE VALVE IS ACTIVE

IN CASE ANY ONE OF THE INDICATIONS TURNS OFF AND THERE IS NO FIRE IN THE COACH PLEASE ISOLATE THE BRAKE VALVE FROM THE MANNUAL ISOLATION AS SHOWN IN THE BELOW FIGURE. THIS WILL ALLOW THE COACH TO BE HAULED.
