#### System Components and Layout
*   **Pantry Car System Components:**
    *   02 nos. point type Heat Detectors (located in hot buffet / pantry area)
    *   Linear Heat Detector (LHD) cable (located on the ceiling of hot buffet / pantry area)
    *   Point type Smoke Detectors (located in manager room, pantry staff room, store room)
    *   Sounder Beacon (located in manager cabin)

---

#### Technical Operational Notes
*   **LHB Hot Buffet/Pantry Car:**
    *   Pre-alarm (smoke detection) and manual activation are available via an activation switch on the control panel.
    *   Upon receiving a Fire alarm signal (via Heat Detectors), the system performs two actions:
        1.  Cuts off power supply to the Electric Hob.
        2.  Initiates automatic fire Suppression system.
*   **ICF Pantry Cars:**
    *   Automatic FDSS operates in manual mode until flame-based cooking is modified to flame-free cooking.
