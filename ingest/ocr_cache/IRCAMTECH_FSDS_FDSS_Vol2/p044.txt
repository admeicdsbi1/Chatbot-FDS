**Heat Detector (Point Type)**

They are installed in the Generator area of the Power car & pantry area of the pantry car.

After pre alarm (smoke detection) manual activation of system is also possible through activation switch provided in control panel.

On receiving fire alarm (through heat detector), the system sends the shut down single to DG set controller through potential free relay contacts and thereafter initiate automatic fire suppression system.

In any case, automatic activation system should be initiated within 30 seconds of sending shut down command to DG set.

**Smoke Detector (Point Type)**

They are installed for detecting smoke in generator area, escorting staff compartment & guard compartment etc. of power car and for detecting smoke in store room, manager room & pantry staff area etc. for pantry car. Smoke detection in these area provides pre alarm at control panel.

**LHD CABLE –**

**Linear Heat Detector** cable installed in the generator area as well as the luggage area of the Power car & in the pantry area of the Pantry car.

LHD cable provides fire alarm as well as initiates actuation of fire suppression system.
