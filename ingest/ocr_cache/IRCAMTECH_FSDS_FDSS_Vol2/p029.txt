### Steps for Isolating the Battery of the Power panel (FDS)

![Power panel interior showing Wago Terminal No 1 and MCB](image_0.png)

* **Wago Terminal No 1** (indicated by red arrow pointing to the terminal block on the left)
* **MCB** (indicated by red arrow pointing to the circuit breaker on the right)
* **Battery Label visible:** CHLOR SAFEPO... Pb

I. Drop the MCB to down position to switch off 110 V power supply
II. Remove Cable form Wago terminal No 1 and apply insulation tape on the exposed end
III. For reinstating the Battery re-join same cable at Wago terminal No 1 and switch on the MCB for reinstating 110 V DC power supply.
