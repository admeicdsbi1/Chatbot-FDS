### Technical Description
*   **Component:** Electrical Cubical (ECC)
*   **Application:** Provided at driving end of MC, TC, NDTC cab.
*   **Function:** Houses all electrical electronics components required for rake level control.
*   **Contents:** MCUS, EBCUS, MCB, Relays, contactor for various application.
