### Component Designations and Descriptions
*   **Key switch:** Used for system activation/deactivation.
*   **Reverser:** Handle for selecting direction (RDM, 0, FWD, REV).
*   **Main handle:** Used for controlling traction and braking (E Brake, FS Brake, COAST, Min Power, Max Power).
