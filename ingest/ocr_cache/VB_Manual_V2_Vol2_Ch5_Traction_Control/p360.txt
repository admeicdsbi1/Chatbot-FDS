### Component Descriptions
*   **DTC GCRW:** Electrical Cubicle located at the guard rear side of the DTC cab.
*   **Function:** Houses electrical and electronics components for rake level control.
*   **Contained Components:** PCU's, EBCU's, CCTV NVRs, CCTV Ethernet Switches, MCB's, Relays, Contactors.
