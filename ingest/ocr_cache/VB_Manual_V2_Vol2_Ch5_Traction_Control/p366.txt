### Technical Content
*   **5.7.19.2 Flasher Light:**
    *   Type: LED
    *   Location: Provided at both the DTC (Driving Trailer Coach).
    *   Control: Control box is provided in the driver's cab.
    *   Function: Attracts the attention of the driver of an approaching train on the adjacent track to indicate caution and to stop the train in the event of unsafe track conditions.

*   **5.7.19.3 Marker Light/Tail Lamp:**
    *   Type: LED
    *   Location: Provided at both the DTC.
