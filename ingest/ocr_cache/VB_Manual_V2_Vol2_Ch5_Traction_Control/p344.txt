**Subject:** Motor Coupling

**Component Description:**
*   **Motor Coupling:** A coupling lubricated by oil, hermetically sealed to the outside by two steel bellows to prevent leakage. It is equipped with drain plugs on both coupling halves to allow for regular oil changes without disassembling the coupling from the shafts.
