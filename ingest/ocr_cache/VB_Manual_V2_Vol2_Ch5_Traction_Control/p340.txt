### Engineering Content Summary

**Rotor Bearing**
*   The motor shaft is equipped with a deep groove ball bearing 6217 at the drive end (DE).
*   The motor shaft is equipped with a cylindrical roller bearing NU 210 at the non-drive end (NDE).

**Traction Motor Cooling System**
*   **Cooling Mechanism:** Airflow is generated by a ventilator directly attached to the motor shaft.
*   **Airflow Path:**
    *   Enters the interior of the machine through the opening in the housing on the non-drive side (NDE).
    *   Drawn over winding overhang and through stator and rotor holes.
    *   Passes the ventilator and leaves the machine in radial direction through air grids on the drive side (DE).
*   **Cooling Configuration:**
    *   Motor is air-cooled by a fan mounted at the non-drive end (NDE) of the rotor shaft.
    *   Airflow is routed through heat exchange channels at the external periphery of the magnetic stack.
    *   A portion of the air is ducted inside the rotor.
    *   Cooling air is filtered at the inlet (grid type).
