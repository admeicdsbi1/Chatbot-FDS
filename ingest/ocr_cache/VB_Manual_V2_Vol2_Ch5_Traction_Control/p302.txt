### Terminal Specifications (from Figure 14)
*   **BN +VE:** M8 26P
*   **BN -VE:** M8 30P

---

### Control Connectors
*   **CON1:** Interface between TCMS and BBU with Ethernet communication.
*   **CON2:** 110 V DC control supply and digital signals.

---

### Component Descriptions
*   **BBU:** Battery Box Unit (Natural-Cooling Unit).
*   **Battery Charging Module:** PWM based IGBT converter.
*   **Battery Charger:** Used to charge 110 V batteries.

---

### Technical Notes
*   **Battery Charging Topology:** Constant voltage constant current limit.
