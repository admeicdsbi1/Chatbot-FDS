### System Descriptions
**5.7.17.2 Driver Display Unit (DDU)**
*   **Function:** Provides information for the TCMS (Train Control and Management System) to support the Loco pilot during normal operation and subsystem malfunctions.
*   **Maintenance Utility:** Supports maintenance staff in setting basic system parameters and fault finding.
*   **Redundancy:** In case of failure of TCMS DDU, the train can still be operated.
*   **Installation:** One DDU installed in each DTC (Driving Trailer Car) cab.
*   **Communication:** Communicates with TCMS.
*   **Basic Functions:**
    *   Provide an interface to control train functions.
    *   Display operational status of train functions.
    *   Display diagnostic events.
*   **Screen Layout:** Consists of 7 sections.
