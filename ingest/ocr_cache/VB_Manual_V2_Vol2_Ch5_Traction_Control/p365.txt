### Component Designations (from diagrams)
The following components are identified in the electrical panel diagrams:

**Upper Panel (Relay/Contactor Panel):**
*   **PCB:** Printed Circuit Board
*   **AUX CONTACTORS:** Auxiliary Contactors
*   **RELAYS:** Relay bank
*   **CONTACTORS:** Contactor bank

**Lower Panel (Circuit Breaker Panel):**
*   **CIRCUIT BREAKERS:** Miniature Circuit Breaker (MCB) distribution panel

**Circuit Breaker Labels (Top to Bottom, Left to Right):**
*   **Row 1:** TC SUPPLY, EPCU SUPPLY, ECN1 SUPPLY, ECN2 SUPPLY, ETB, EBCU1 SUPPLY, EBCU2 SUPPLY
*   **Row 2:** DCU LEFT SUPPLY, DCU RIGHT SUPPLY, ACU, LINE ISOLATOR SUPPLY, PANTOGRAPH SUPPLY, PB SUPPLY, EMT BRAKE SUPPLY, PAS SUPPLY
*   **Row 3:** PIS SUPPLY, ICD CB, DBDS CB, CCTV MCB, ILL1 SUPPLY, ILL2 SUPPLY, ILL3 SUPPLY, ILL4 SUPPLY
*   **Row 4:** AAC SUPPLY, HLD, ACU/2

### Technical Description
*   **Component:** Twin LED type headlights
*   **Location:** Provided at both the DTC (Driving Trailer Car)
*   **Function:** Illuminates front track ahead at night time or during daytime when passing through dark places (e.g., tunnels). Facilitates visibility for the train pilot to identify obstructions or track abnormalities.
