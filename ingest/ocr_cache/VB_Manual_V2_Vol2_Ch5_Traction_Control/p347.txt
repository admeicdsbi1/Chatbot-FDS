5.7.14 Jumper Couplers

Jumper coupler (Power coupler) is used for transmission of power supply from one coach to the other coach. The Unit is mounted directly to under-frame of MC and TC coaches. Power coupler assembly consists of three sub assemblies:
• Junction box
• Harting connector plate
• Cable holding plate
