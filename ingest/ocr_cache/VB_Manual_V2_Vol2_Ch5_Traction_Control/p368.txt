### Component Specifications

| Component | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wiper Arm Assembly** | Manufactured from stainless steel with brass castings, polyester powder coated. Mounts directly onto spindles protruding through the cab structure. Secured with nuts and washers. |
| **Wiper Blade** | Secured to the arm assembly using a blade clip arrangement with a blade retaining screw and Nylock nut. |
| **Wash Tank/Pump Assembly** | 9.6L stainless steel tank. Mounted via four slotted brackets. Includes a 24v DC powered pump mounted inside the tank. |
