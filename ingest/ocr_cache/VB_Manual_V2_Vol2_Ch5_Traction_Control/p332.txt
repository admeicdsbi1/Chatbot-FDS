### Technical Index: VBE Trainset Maintenance

**Document Reference**
*   **Subject:** Maintenance Manual of VBE Trainset (V2.0)
*   **Reference Number:** IRCAMTECH/GWL/2022-23/T-18/EL/2.0
*   **Page Number:** 52 of 123
*   **Section:** 5. Maintenance

**Component Designations and Descriptions**
*   **Coolers:** Made of aluminium plates and bars; used for cooling oil.
*   **Motor fan sets:** Used to suck heated air and blow it out the bottom side.

**Technical Values**
*   **Voltage:** 415 V
*   **Phases:** Three phases
*   **Frequency:** 50Hz

**Operational Process (Coolers and motor fan sets)**
1.  Fresh air is obtained from the external side of the car body.
2.  Air passes through coolers (made of aluminium plates and bars) to cool the oil inside.
3.  Heated air is sucked by the motor fan and blown out the bottom side.
4.  Motor fan sets are connected to the train via a Harting connector.
