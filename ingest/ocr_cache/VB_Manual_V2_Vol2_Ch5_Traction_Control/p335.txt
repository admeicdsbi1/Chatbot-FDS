### Component Index

| Component Designation | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **PT100 sensors** | Two sensors mounted on the tank. Signals are sent to the supervision system to regulate oil temperature via fan motor speed. |
| **Oil flow indicator** | A paddle-type device that detects minimal oil flow to monitor oil circulation and proper functioning of the oil pump. |
| **Pressure relief device** | Also referred to as an Overpressure valve. |

### Technical Values
*   **PT100:** Sensor type designation.
