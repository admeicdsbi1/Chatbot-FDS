### Technical Components and Descriptions

**Air Dryer**
*   **Function:** Dehumidifies air entering or leaving the transformer's oil expansion tank to prevent over or under pressure due to oil volume changes.
*   **Configuration:**
    *   First air dryer: Connected to the oil expansion tank by the air dryer pipe.
    *   Second air dryer: Placed on the LV bushings box to dry the air inside the box.

**Bushings**
*   **High voltage plug-in bushing (1):** 25kV input.
*   **Ceramic bushings (2):** 13 nos. total (12 low voltage outputs, 1 neutral input).
