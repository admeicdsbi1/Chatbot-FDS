5.7.4.1 Brake Chopper Description

There are two sets of resistor branches connected in series (their equivalent resistances are R1 and R2) between DC link terminals. R1 and R2 are different resistances each of values 66 kΩ and 20.4 kΩ respectively.

* Brake Chopper unit is mounted directly to under frame.
* Unit outline dimensions are 884 mm(L) x 648 mm (W) x 475 mm (H).
* Unit Max. weight is 100 Kgs.
