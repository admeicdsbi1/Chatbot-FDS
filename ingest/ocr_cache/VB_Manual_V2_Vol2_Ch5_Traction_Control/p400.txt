5.7.27.10 Saloon and Cab Loudspeakers
➢ Speaker of 6 watts r.m.s. rating of reputed make Ahu a is provided
➢ 50% of sharing is provided for the speakers between coaches in case of single power
➢ amplifier failure at least half of the speakers are still operative in the coach
➢ Speakers are distributed in equal distance diagonally for even coverage of the sound in the coach.

5.7.27.11 Microphone
➢ Microphone is a professional high quality goose neck microphone are suitable for various PA applications
➢ Microphone is interfaced to MCP for PA, IC, ETBU operations
➢ Driver can talk through the microphone.

5.7.27.12 GPS Module
➢ A rugged GPS module with inbuilt antenna is located on the roof of DTC
➢ GPS module has RS485 communication with MCP unit for acquiring latitude, longitude, Date time information.

5.7.28 Driver’s Cab Air Conditioner
This is roof mounted direct expansion type packaged unit, provided for Driver’s cab in DTC.
