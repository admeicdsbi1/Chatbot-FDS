### Technical Index: TCAS System

**Subject:** TCAS (Train Collision Avoidance System)

**System Overview**
*   **Function:** Supporting system to loco pilot; intervenes only when the loco pilot is unable to react to unsafe situations.
*   **Operational Responsibility:** Loco pilot remains responsible for running the train and must continue to follow existing operational procedures, general and subsidiary rules of the Railway, including obeying line-side signals and all temporary/permanent speed restrictions.

**Salient Features of TCAS System**
*   **Speed Supervision:** Monitors current speed against Maximum Permitted Speed (MPS).
*   **Warning System:** Provides automatic visual and audio warnings during over-speed conditions.
*   **Brake Application Threshold:** Automatic brake application occurs if current speed exceeds the MPS by more than 5 kmph.
*   **Rollaway/Rollback Protection:** If the train rolls back in reverse direction in normal mode for more than a configurable small distance, brakes are applied to stop the train and released once the train is stopped.
*   **Collision/Incident Prevention:**
    *   Prevention of Signal Passing at Danger (SPAD).
    *   Prevention of side collision in block section.
    *   Prevention of head-on and rear-end collisions.

**Technical Values**
*   **Brake Application Threshold:** > 5 kmph over MPS
