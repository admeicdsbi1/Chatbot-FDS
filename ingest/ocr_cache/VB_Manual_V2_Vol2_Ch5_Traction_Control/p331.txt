### Technical Specifications
*   **Isolating Valves:** DN80
*   **Oil Pump Flow Rate:** > 35 m³/h
*   **Oil Pump Power Supply:** 415 V, three-phase, 50Hz

### Component Designations and Descriptions
*   **Container:** Set on the air link between air dryer and conservator tank to avoid air dryer pollution with oil.
*   **Oil sight glass:** Placed on the container to check the presence of oil.
*   **Cooler:** Equipped with 2 motor fans; fed with oil by oil pump.
*   **Isolating valves (DN80):** Used to isolate the refrigeration assembly to allow for cooler replacement while limiting oil drainage.
*   **Immersion type pump:** Ensures the flow of oil; starts as soon as the railcar is powered up.
*   **Terminal box:** Connection point for the oil pump power supply.
