Annexure-1

Steps to follow for Isolating the Battery of the Power panel ( FDS)

[Image: A photograph of an electrical panel containing batteries, a Wago terminal block, and a Schneider MCB.]

- Wago Terminal No 1
- MCB

Picture -1

1. Isolate the Brake Valve pneumatically situated on the underframe. As shown in the Picture Below

[Image: Two photos showing a valve handle. Left: "HANDLE POSITION TO OPEN" with a yellow arrow pointing right. Right: "HANDLE POSITION TO ISOLATE BRAKE VALVE" with a yellow arrow showing a clockwise rotation.]

2. Drop the MCB to down Position to switch off 110 V power Supply
3. Remove Cable form Wago terminal No 1 and apply insulation tape on the exposed end.
4. For reinstating the Battery re join same cable at Wago terminal No 1 and switch on the MCB for reinstating 110 V DC power supply.
