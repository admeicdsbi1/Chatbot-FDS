Sub: Testing of fire properties for coach furnishing Materials.
Ref: (i) This office letter no. MC/ACF/Fire Accident dated 02.09.2014.

Vide this office letter referred above, list of external laboratories for testing of fire retardancy properties of coach furnishing material was issued to Zonal Railways. In addition to above, RDSO has audited three Labs viz. CIPET/Chennai, CIPET/Bhubaneswar & CPRI/Bangalore for testing of fire retardancy properties at their lab premises.

After successful audit by RDSO, these three labs has been included in the previous list issued vide letter under reference (i). Amended list is attached for your reference.

It is further informed that M&C directorate of RDSO has also test facilities for four fire properties i.e. Resistance to Spread of Flame, LOI, and Deterioration in visibility due to Smoke & Toxicity. Therefore samples can also be sent to M&C Directorate of RDSO occasionally.
