Sub : Testing of Fire Properties for coach furnishing materials.
Ref : This office letter of even no. dated 01.04.2019

Vide reference above, the list of external laboratories for testing of coach furnishing materials had been issued to Zonal Railways / Production Units covering testing for the following fire properties:
a. Toxicity as per specification No. NCD-1409
b. Limiting Oxygen Index IS-13501/IS-13360
c. Deterioration of visibility due to smoke as per specification No. UIC-564-2 OR
d. Resistance to spread of Flame as per specification No. UIC-564-2 OR

Over the years, specifications for materials have been progressively improved. RDSO specifications for coach furnishing materials now also include of testing of Heat Release Rate property (HRR) as per EN 45545-2 with test method as per ISO-5660-1.
