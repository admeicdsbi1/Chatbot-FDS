NOTE:-
1. ITEM DETAILS
a. MATERIAL FOR RAMP IS ALUMINIUM AL6061 T6.
b. END CAP & LOCK FOR RAMP IS THE WHEEL CHAIR IS DURABLE PLASTIC CONSTRUCTION, WHICH IS RUST PROOF AND SCRATCH PROOF.
c. RAMP SURFACE HAS ANTI-SLIP MAT SURFACE PATTERN TO INCREASE FRICTION AND ENHANCE THE ANTI SLIP PERFORMANCE.
d. LOCK HAND GUARD BUTTON TO BE PROVIDED TO PREVENT HAND PINCHING WHEN TELESCOPING.
e. RAMP BODY IS POWDER COATED, WHICH IS UV RESISTANT, WEATHERPROOF, DURABLE, RUST PROOF AND SCRATCH PROOF.
f. NECESSARY PROVISION SHALL BE PROVIDED FOR EASY TO GRASP AND EASY TO CARRY.
g. LOAD CARRYING CAPACITY OF RAM - 300KG.
h. PRODUCT WEIGHT - 6.5 KG(APP)
2. 2 Nos OF LOCATOR PIN & BUSH ASSY FOR EACH RAMP (ON EITHER SIDE OF DOORWAY) SHALL BE IN THE SCOPE OF RAMP SUPPLIER.
3. LOCATOR PIN SHALL BE RIVETTED WITH RAMP.
4. LOCATOR BUSH TO BE PASTED BY USING WELDON/PLEXUS AND SCREWED ON FLOOR RIGIDLY TO FIX THE RAMP WITHOUT ANY SHAKE.
5. SEE DETAILED NOTES, DIMENSIONS GIVEN IN MM UNLESS SPECIFIED OTHERWISE.
6. IN IDLE CONDITION, RAMPS TO BE TIED & PLACED IN POSITION AS LOCATION SHOWN IN THE DRG. RAMP TO BE TIED WITH 2 SETS OF NYLON BELT WITH BUCKLE(MALE & FEMALE) BELT TO BE SECURED WITH SS BRACKET IN FLOOR.
7. DIMENSIONS GIVEN IN THE DRG. ARE INDICATIVE ONLY.
