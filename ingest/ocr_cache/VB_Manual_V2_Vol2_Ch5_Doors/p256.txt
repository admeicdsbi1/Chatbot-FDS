### Technical Index: VBE Trainset Maintenance

**Document Information**
*   **Subject:** Replacement of carriage 1
*   **Section:** 5. Maintenance – Automatic Doors
*   **Manual:** Maintenance Manual of VBE Trainset (V2.0)

**Component Designations**
*   **Carrier assy:** Smallest replaceable unit for carriage 1

**Maintenance Procedures**

**Disassembly**
1.  Loosen the belt tension.
2.  Unscrew the end stop of the guiding profile.
3.  Unscrew the T-profile of the right and left basis.
4.  Move the carriers to the free end of the guide tube and remove it through the opening of the profile.

**Installation**
*   Assembly takes place in reverse order.

**Safety Notes**
*   The carriage can only be replaced if the door leaf is disengaged.
*   The end stop in door-opened position has to be disassembled.
*   Observe the following items:
    1) Put down the eccentric axis.
