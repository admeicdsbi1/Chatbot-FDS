### Technical Values

*   **Connector:** X7

### Component Designations
*   **Eccentric bolt:** Adjustment component for the threaded pin and eccentric axis.
*   **Allen Key in threaded pin:** Tool interface for adjusting the threaded pin.
*   **Actuator:** The smallest replaceable unit for the automatic door system.
*   **Toothed belt:** Component requiring tension adjustment on the carriage.
*   **Motor:** Component mounted on the guide tube.
*   **Control unit:** Electrical interface for the motor connector.

### Maintenance Procedures

#### Disassembly
1.  Open the control unit and remove the motor connector X7.
2.  Loosen the belt tension on the carriage until the toothed belt can be removed from the motor.
3.  Unscrew the motor on the guide tube (2 Nuts M8).

#### Installation
1.  Assembly takes place in reverse order, cutting the motor cable to the required length.
2.  Place sleeves on the cable ends and fasten the cable to the motor using cable ties.
3.  Readjust the belt tension after mounting and perform a functional test.
