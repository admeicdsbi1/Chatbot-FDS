**Component Designations**
*   **Carrier assy:** Smallest replaceable unit for carriage 2.
*   **Centric:** Roller type (indicated on carrier assembly).
*   **Eccentric:** Roller type (indicated on carrier assembly).
*   **Tensioning device:** Mechanism for adjusting belt tension.

**Technical Values**
*   **Torque:** 14 Nm

**Maintenance Procedures**

**Replacement of carriage 2**
*   **Disassembly:**
    1.  Loosen the belt tension.
    2.  Unscrew the actuator of the guiding profile.
    3.  Move the carrier to the free end of the guide tube and remove it through the opening of the profile.
*   **Installation:**
    *   Assembly takes place in reverse order.
