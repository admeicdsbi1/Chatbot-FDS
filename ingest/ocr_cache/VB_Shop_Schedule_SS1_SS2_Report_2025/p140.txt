iv. Preventive maintenance contract (PMC):
For critical/niche items provided in Vande-Bharat, this concept has been adopted to facilitate regular preventive maintenance through OEM/outsourced agency even during warranty period.

v. Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contract (CAMC)- For critical/niche items, Comprehensive maintenance contract has been adopted ensuring regular scheduled maintenance with supply of related materials/consumables etc. This has been adopted for period beyond warranty.

vi. Rate contracts:
It has been decided to mandate for execution of 02 types of rate contracts:
A. By ICF for:
a. supply of complete assembly/sub-assembly/components
b. Major schedules of assembly/sub-assembly
c. Refurbishment/major repair of the assembly/sub-assembly.

B. By Zonal Railways for:
a. touch & feel items required to maintain overall aesthetics of the coach interior like Floor Mat/Cover, Windows and Roller Blinds etc.
b. Works and services like repair and refurbishment of window glass/Driver look out glass etc.

vii. Annual Maintenance cum operation contract (AMOC):
For items like bio toilets, AMOCs has been adopted to facilitate smooth maintenance.

The above strategy has been adopted for all items of Vande Bharat as per technical and operational requirement related to these items. As per requirement the system wise/item wise details has been studied and for each items maintenance scheme has been suggested as enclosed under Annexure II.

For any left-over items the similar strategy may further be adopted as per requirement.

Note: In all of the above cases, the warranty claims will continue to be lodged and concluded as per applicable guidelines.
