However, in view of comprehensive maintenance policy issued for maintenance of different systems/subsystems/components of IR manufactured Vande Bharat Trainsets vide letter reference (ii) and revised list as submitted by ICF vide letter reference (iii), list of unit exchange spares has been further reviewed. Accordingly, list of unit exchange spares has been updated and enclosed as Annexure-I.

Further, GM/ICF is authorized to enhance the quantity of unit exchange spares per set, if required, in view of continued augmentation of Vande Bharat Rakes over IR.

In this regard, it is advised to procure all Unit Exchange Spares and supply to Zonal Railways utilizing existing RSP P.B. Item No. 1248/23-24 within the sanctioned amount under intimation to this office.
