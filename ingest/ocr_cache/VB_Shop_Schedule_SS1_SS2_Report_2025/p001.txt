Sub: Report of the committee constituted for defining maintenance activities in
SS-I and SS-II Schedule of Vande Bharat rakes.
Ref: (i) Railway Board letter no ERB-I/2025/23/15 dated 02.04.2025
(ii) CAMTECH letter no. IRCAMTECH/GWL/M/Trainset/SS-I&SS-II dt 24.07.2025

A committee was constituted vide reference (i) above for defining the maintenance
schedules for SS-II schedule and updating the maintenance manual by incorporating additional
maintenance activities for SS-I schedules of Vande Bharat Rakes and other related activities.

The committee vide its letter under reference (ii) above has submitted the final report
along with recommendations, based on the latest OEMs guidelines, suggestions and feedback
from RDSO, ICF and Zonal Railways. The final report of the committee has been examined and
accordingly approved by Board (MTRS) and attached as Annexure-I.

All Zonal Railways and nominated workshops are hereby advised to adhere to the
maintenance guidelines and ensure strict compliance of the recommendations to facilitate the
seamless and reliable maintenance of Vande Bharat rakes during various maintenance
schedules like SS-I and SS-II schedules.

For further necessary action please.
