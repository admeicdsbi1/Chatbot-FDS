Sub: Comprehensive Maintenance Policy for IR manufactured Vande Bharat Trainsets.

In view of accelerated induction of Vande Bharat Trainsets with niche technologies and to ensure higher standards of its maintenance, upkeep and ensuring maximum availability, a comprehensive maintenance policy for Vande Bharat Trains has been deliberated at Board in consultation with finance.

The detailed maintenance philosophy as approved alongwith detailed major system wise maintenance scheme is enclosed as Annexure I and Annexure-II respectively.

This will further be reviewed based on experience gained by different Zonal Railways in future.

In this context, following action should be taken by ICF and Zonal Railways:-

1. ICF is nominated to: -

a. Enter into Preventive Maintenance Contracts (during warranty period) and Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts for 05 years (beyond warranty period) for respective systems/subsystems/components as indicated under Annexure-II.
