GUI INSTRUCTIONS
1. SELECT THE COM.
2. PRESS THE SELECT PORT BUTTON AND WAIT FOR 2 SECONDS UNTIL THE LED STATUS IS GREEN.
3. ENTER THE COACH NUMBER.
4. ENTER THE COMMAND TO RETRIEVE THE DATA.
4. PRESS THE REPORT BUTTON ACCORDINGLY.
5. FINALLY CLICK THE STOP BUTTON TO EXIT FROM EXE.
