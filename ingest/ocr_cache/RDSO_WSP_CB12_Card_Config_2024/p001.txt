Sub: Configuring of CB-12 card in M/s KBI make Wheel Slide Protection (WSP).

In technical seminars held at Secunderabad and Guwahati it was reported that some of the new coaches received in coaching depots are found without configuring of the CB-12 card in M/s KBI Wheel Slide Protection (WSP) system, which results in error/event data not being logged.

In this regard it is requested that the configuring of CB-12 card must be ensured by all Production Units at the time of installation of the system.

Accordingly, a specific check sheet must be added in WSP test of coaches in PU for ensuring proper configuration of WSP of all makes as per OEM instructions.
