Sub: Attention to arrest leakages in Air Suspension System of Vande Bharat coaches.

Ref: RDSO letter no. MC/TS/ BD dated 27.12.2023

Vide RDSO letter under reference, instructions have already been issued to Zonal Railways and PUs for implementation to arrest the leakages in air suspension system of Vande Bharat (VB) coaches and to send the feedback/ observations to RDSO for further review.

MMCT depot-WR and RKMP depot-WCR, have taken appreciable initiative in this regard by using air spring pressure readings from REMMLOT data for monitoring of the health of air suspension system & have provided a directory of identified root causes of leakages in air suspension system. The same is enclosed herewith as Annexure-1 for easy identification of probable source of leakage in air suspension system in Vande Bharat coaches.

In view of the above, it is again requested for compliance of instructions issued vide letter under reference. Annexure-1 of this letter may be utilized for the purpose & similar feedback with identified root-cause of leakage in air suspension system may be provided to RDSO for further circulation to all.
