Sub: Periodicity of refurbishment of CTRBs.
Ref: (i) Carriage Workshop Alambagh letter No. M/CWM/AMV/152/RDSO/CNL dated 27.06.2024.
(ii) This office letter no MC/CTRB/Genl. Dated 01.01.2020

1. Vide clause no. 10.6 of CAMTECH maintenance manual, CTRB should be sent for refurbishment/reconditioning in SS-II schedule (36 months).

2. Further, vide this office letter under ref (ii), mounting date of CTRBs on LHB wheel sets should be used for assessing the refurbishment schedule (03 years of period) of CTRBs.

3. Vide letter under reference(i), Alambagh Workshop/NR has mentioned that wheelsets received from division for SS-1 schedule have generally run in service for 19 to 21 months in place of exactly 18 months. In such cases, workshop dismounts the bearing in SS-I schedule to comply with the refurbishment schedule of 36 months of CTRB.

4. Moreover, as per CAMTECH maintenance manual, SS-I schedule should be done in 18 Months+30 days/6 lakhs Kms whichever is earlier. And SS-II schedule should be done in 36 months/12 Lakh kilometre whichever is earlier.

In view of the above, the refurbishment schedule of CTRBs on LHB coaches are revised to 36+3 months or 12 lakh kilometre whichever is earlier.
