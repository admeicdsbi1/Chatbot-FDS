11. Detail procedure for the modifications:

1. Objective:
To facilitate access to the VCB equipment for periodic maintenance by installing VCB Hatch Doors in coaches where pre-cut openings in the FRP ceiling panel are not available. This instruction mandates cut-away operations followed by fixing of hatch door as per the reference drawings.

2. Material/Consumables required
As per the drawings in table-1.

3. Tools required
1. Measuring tape and marking tools.
2. Spirit Level- To ensure proper alignment of door frame.
3. Portable Hand Drill / Drill Machine(preferably battery operated, if power supply/pneumatic supply is not available nearby)- For drilling holes for fixing hatch frame
4. Center Punch & Hammer- For precise hole marking before drilling.
5. Pop riveting gun (preferably battery operated, if power supply/pneumatic supply is not available nearby).
6. Torque Wrench / Nut Driver Set- For tightening fasteners to specified torque.
7. Disc cutter (preferably battery operated, if power supply/pneumatic supply is not available nearby) - For cutting FRP panel as per required cut-out.
8. Deburring Tool / File Set- To smoothen cut edges of FRP after cutting
9. Silicone Gun / Sealant Applicator- For applying gasket/sealant around hatch frame
10. Tool kit- for removal/ installation of roof ceiling panel
11. PPE kit

4. Safety precautions to be observed
1. Ensure that OHE is down, rake is in de-energized condition
2. Wear Personal Protective Equipments.

5. Steps to be followed.

| Step No | Instruction | Remarks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Step 1 | Mark the cut-away location for VCB doors on FRP ceiling panels as per Drg. No. 891-4-4-017 | Ensure door cut-away location matches with the VCB equipment accessing area. |
| Step 2 | Remove the FRP ceiling panels at the end wall and pantry area. | Remove all the electrical connections properly, if any. Keep the panel at a safe location with suitable identification marks |
