Stage - 2 of Smoke / Fire Detection

4. When the smoke reaches the threshold of Stage – 2, Fire (1.6 ± 0.05 % obs/m, Delay – 45 seconds) in the Automatic Smoke / Fire Detection System, the below-mentioned indications will be observed in the coach and Driver Console in DTC.

a. LCD indication on the Fire Panel.

b. LED indication on the Aspirating Smoke Detection Unit.

[Image labels: HOCHIKI, FIRE, FAULT, OK, FIRElink]

c. The TCMS Driver Display Unit in the Driver Console/Desk in DTC will display the fire detection indication symbol in Red Color and the corresponding coach diagnostic message will be displayed.

[Table/Display labels: UNIT1, UNIT2, UNIT5, UNIT6, Cab, VCB, Loop, Em Stop, M1 LC, ACM1, TCMS, M2 LC, ACM2, ACC, Fire-2 Detected, U1, DTC, PCU, 4127, U1, CCU, Holding Brake Disabled from DDU, Acknowledge, Zero Force]

d. MIN 1 FD lamp indication light will be illuminated in the MCP Unit Display Panel of the Driver Console/Desk in DTC.

[Image labels: ON, MIN1 FD/ACK]
