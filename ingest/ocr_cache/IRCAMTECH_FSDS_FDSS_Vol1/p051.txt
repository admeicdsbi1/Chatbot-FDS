II. Aerosol Generator with BTA Activation (FIREPRO)
* Before installation, inspect the integrity of the aerosol generator.
* The installation parameters are to be observed according to the manufacturer's recommendations. (Please refer to Table-8 above for details)
* Position the mounting bracket and securely fasten it to the cabinet or wall in a manner that ensures that the aerosol generator is not subjected to accidental damage or movement.
* Fasten the fixing bracket on the body of the aerosol generator, around mid-height.
