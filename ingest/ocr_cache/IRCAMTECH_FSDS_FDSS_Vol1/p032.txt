4.1.2.5. Cleaning
1. Control Panel / Detection Panel
• Clean the outer side of the panel by using a lint-free cloth with Isopropyl alcohol.
• The interior of the panel should be cleaned using vacuuming.

2. Aspirating Smoke Detection Unit
External cleaning of the unit should be performed using a damp (not wet) lint-free cloth with Isopropyl alcohol.

Caution: Do not use solvents as these may mar the front panel label.
