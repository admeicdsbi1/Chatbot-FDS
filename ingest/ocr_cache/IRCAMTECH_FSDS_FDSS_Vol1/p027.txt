4.1.2. Maintenance and Testing

4.1.2.1. Smoke Test
1. Conduct smoke test by simulating smoke generation from any source e.g. smoke generator at the farthest end from the system and verify detector performance.

2. When the smoke reaches the threshold of Alert (0.35 ± 0.05 % obs/m, Delay – 20 seconds) in the Automatic Smoke / Fire Detection System, the system will display LCD indications on the Fire Panel of the coach.
