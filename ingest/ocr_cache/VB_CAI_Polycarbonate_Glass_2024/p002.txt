To avoid the breakage of the polycarbonate glass during maintenance, fixed door is changed to hinged door (Acrylic sheet) for better maintainability of Fire extinguisher.
Tools required:
1. Pneumatic Drilling machine
2. Screw driver
Items required:
1. Hinged door (Acrylic sheet) as per dimensions (Item-1)
2. Knob (Item-2)
3. Hinge (Item-3)
4. Magnetic Catch
Steps to be followed:
1. Remove the front glass of the fire extinguisher without damaging the FRP panel.
2. Clean the area with proper cleaners
3. Assemble the Hinges and glass door as per drawing no. 890-6-4-025 alt ‘a’
