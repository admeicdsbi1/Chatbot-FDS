ANNEXURE

1. It is a general practice to reclose VCB by LP whenever “not all VCB closed” indication is noticed
2. Anyways, Repeated Reclosing of VCB by LP in case of tripping occurred by “Transformer primary over current fault” lead to repeated arcing at fault location and subsequent fire safety related issues . It is therefore instructed to LP/ALP to restrain from reclosing the VCB in case of “ VCB tripping by – transformer primary over current fault” and follow the procedure B to isolate the BU in this particular case.
3. The procedure for checking the fault on which the VCB is tripped is explained in procedure A.

Procedure A : Confirmation of “Transformer primary over current fault”.

a) Check if “NOT ALL VCB CLOSED” Indication is ON in Indication Panel,

Panel Components:
- PANTO 1,4 UP (Yellow)
- NOT ALL VCB CLOSED (Yellow)
- EMR BRAKE (Red)
- EMR OFF (Red)
- MIN 1 PB APPLIED (Red)
- MIN 1 AS FAILED (Blue)
- MIN 1 BR APPLIED (Red)
- MIN 1 DOOR OPENED (Red)
- AC FAULT (Red)
- PANTO 2,3 UP (Red)
- MIN 1 VCB CLOSED (Blue)
- RDM (Blue)
- OHE AVAILABLE (Green)
- ALL DOORS CLOSED (Green)
- SINGLE UNIT OPERATION (Yellow)
- TCN FAILURE (Red)
