Ensuring integrity of Electrical connections of WSP system was also reiterated in RDSO letters at Ref (2) and (3).

It is noted from the WSP fault / event data downloaded by Zonal Railways that several faults indicating short circuit / broken cable are repeatedly being set and cleared in the error logs. These intermittent faults arise mainly due to loose / damaged electrical connections in the WSP system. In many cases, the same faults are repeating in subsequent journeys even after maintenance. As such, Zonal Railways may ensure that:

1. Proper troubleshooting is carried out to arrive at the root cause of the recorded fault and any loose / damaged electrical connections are identified and attended to. It may be ensured that the same faults do not repeat after maintenance.

2. A one-time drive may be carried out in coaching depots to ensure vibration resistant electrically tight connections in the WSP wiring system.
