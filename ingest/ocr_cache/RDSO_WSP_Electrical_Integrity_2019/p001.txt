Sub: Ensuring Integrity of electrical connections of WSP System
Ref: 1) RDSO Letter No. MC/LHB/Brake dt. 28-03-2018
2) RDSO letter no. MC/LHB/Brake dated 27.09.2018
3) RDSO letter no. MC/LHB/Brake dt. 15.04.2019

RDSO vide letters at Ref (1) and (2) above had investigated wheel shelling in LHB coaches and issued recommendations to Zonal Railways / PUs to reduce wheel shelling. Consolidated instructions for addressing the problem of Wheel Shelling in LHB Coaches were also issued to Zonal Railways / PUs vide Ref (3).

Improper / Loose electrical connections of WSP system have been identified as one of the major causes of wheel shelling. In this connection, extract of para 2.3.1 at Ref(1) [Carriage Research Report RDSO/CG/CRR-18001 dated 28.03.2018] is reproduced below:

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2.3.1a | A one-time drive needs to be carried out in coaching depots to ensure vibration resistant electrically tight connections in the WSP wiring system. |
| 2.3.1b | Enquiries at several coaching depots bring out that the WSP wiring attention is carried out only by OEM under AMC. A feedback system needs to be formalized in maintenance depots whereby the SSE / JE in-charge of maintenance ensures that faults logged by the WSP processor of M/s FTIL and the fault counter readings for the WSP processor of M/s KBIL are acted upon and do not repeat in the next trip. The present practice of noting only the fault code of 95/99 in the WSP processor display (for eg. as brought out in Annexure A) is inadequate as these codes are displayed even when intermittent faults occur and are subsequently cleared, such as due to loose wiring. |
