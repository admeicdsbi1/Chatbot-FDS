4.3 Construction

The AEF G2 WSP control unit is composed of a set of standard GEMINI2 electronic boards connected at the rear by a motherboard, and at the front to the customer low voltage side.

The Electronics rack contains the electronic devices dedicated to wheel slide protection and additional functions. The link between the car wiring and the electronic unit is realized by six cable connectors which are located on the frontside of the case. A further plug, RS232, enables computer access for maintenance, service and system diagnosis.
