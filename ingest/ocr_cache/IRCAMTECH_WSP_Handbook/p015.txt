**MV – Dump valve amplifier:**

[Image: A rack-mounted electronic module labeled "SWKP AS 20 R" by SAB WABCO. The module has several slots labeled N1, N2, OP, R, MV, ST, CPU, RÜ, GE 1, GE 2. A yellow arrow points to the "MV" slot.]

*   4 dump valves each amplifier board
*   4 independent safety circuits
*   both-sides switch-off of solenoids in the case of the watchdog time-out
*   protection against revers polarity and short circuit proof
*   circuit-brake detection
*   24 V supply voltage for solenoids, independent on different board voltage

This module is used for controlling the dump valves via bipolar output amplifier.
