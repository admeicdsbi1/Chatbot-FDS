1.6 Details of KNORR BREMSE WSP Systems:
This electronic unit (MGS2 WSP System) is mounted in a rake case & housed in the electrical controlled panel. This system consists of following parts:

1.6.1. Power board:
The power boards are fitted in a closed box. Its front panel has two yellow LED’s indicating the operating state. This board supplies the voltage for powering the boards, actuators and speed sensors. For powering the anti-skid valve a 24 V source is used from MGS2 control unit. Input Supply voltage – 24V ± 30% DC to 110 ± 30% DC.
