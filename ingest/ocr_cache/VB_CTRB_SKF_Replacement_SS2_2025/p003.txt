Sub: SKF CTRB failures in Vande Bharat coaches
Ref: RDSO letter No: MC/WA/Genl. Dated: 04.07.2025

Three hot Axle case have been reported in Vande Bharat coaches within a short span of two months. one of these incidents resulted in significant passenger inconvenience due to the train being short terminated enroute. All the affected coaches were equipped with CTRBs manufactured by M/s SKF India.

2. In addition, during the SS-1 maintenance schedule in workshops, 20 CTRBs of M/s SKF India make were prematurely removed due to multiple issues, including grease oozing, high mounted end play (MEP), abnormal noise, etc. These issues present a high potential for hot axle occurrences in the future, which is a matter of serious concern.

3. In light of these developments, the Railway Board convened review meetings on 27.06.2025 and 01.07.2025, wherein it was directed that urgent corrective and preventive measures must be undertaken to prevent recurrence of such failures.

4. Subsequently, M/s SKF India has conducted preliminary investigations on a few failed bearings and observed spalling on the outer cup raceways. The suspected root cause has been attributed to ovality in the outer cup raceway. In response, SKF has shifted from sample-based measurement to 100% measurement of ovality. However, despite ongoing investigations, no substantive corrective action has been implemented at the ground level to prevent further failures.

5. Recently, Sonipat Workshop has also reported abnormal sound in two CTRBs of SKF make during SS-1 maintenance, further highlighting the persisting issues.

6. Vide reference cited above, RDSO has already advised a series of actions to be undertaken by M/s SKF India. However, no appreciable effort appears to have been made by the firm, and the issues with the bearings continue without resolution.

In view of the above and considering the gravity of the issue, particularly its potential impact on passenger inconvenience, operational reliability, and traffic management, it is advised to withhold procurement and further fitment of SKF make CTRBs for Vande Bharat version-2 coaches until further instructions are issued by RDSO or ICF.
