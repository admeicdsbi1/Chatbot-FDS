1. SEALING OF DRIVER CAB CRW PANEL DOOR AND TCAS PANEL DOOR IN DTC COACHES.

Reference Drg No: 889 56 002 & 889 56 003

Coaches: Applicable for all Train set DTC coaches

Items required:
1. Rubber profile L-7800
2. Adhesive to ICF/MD/Spec 093.

TCAS
CRW
CRW PANEL

Tools required:
1. Knife.
2. Measuring Tape.
3. Cotton waste.
4. Putty blade-2 inches, for applying adhesive

Steps to be followed:
1. Sealing rubber profile shall be inserted to the door panel edges all around and is to be pasted using adhesive to ICF/MD/Spec 093.
2. Ensure that the doors are closing and opening without any difficulty.
3. Ensure that necessary tightness is there so that the door is air tight.
