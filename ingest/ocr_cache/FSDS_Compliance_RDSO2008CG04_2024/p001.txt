Sub: Compliance of RDSO specification no. RDSO/2008/CG-04 and Guidelines for FSDS system.
Ref: This office letter of even no. dated: 12.08.2024 & 08.10.2024.

Vide letter under reference, guidelines for provision of circuit breaker, segregation of +ve/-ve wire etc. were issued for earliest completion, however few Zonal Railways have informed that Suppliers are not carryingout these activities.

In this regard, it is again reiterated that in reference to subject of supplier's responsibility for safety of passengers & equipments and provision of MCB, clauses are already specified in the RDSO specification no. RDSO/2008/CG-04 for FSDS system.

As per Clause 8.1 of Section-A of RDSO specification no. RDSO/2008/CG-04:
"The supplier/OEM shall be completely responsible for the execution of the contract strictly in accordance with the terms of this specification and the conditions of contract, not withstanding any approval which purchaser or the Inspecting Officer may have given".

Additionally, as per Clause 8.4(Section-A) of RDSO specification no. RDSO/2008/CG-04:
"For ensuring that the equipments/systems do not lead to unsafe or hazardous conditions for IR passengers & equipments".

The specification clearly mandates that suppliers are bound to adhere to all requirements to ensure safety, reliability, and strict compliance of specification.

Furthermore, as per Clause no. 1.7 (a) of Section-B of RDSO specification no. RDSO/2008/CG-04(Rev-5 onwards), it is categorically stated that, "The control supply to the Fire Detection System will be provided from Terminal Block (TB) X1 of the Switch Board Cabinet (SBC) for LHB AC coaches through DC MCB of adequate rating".
