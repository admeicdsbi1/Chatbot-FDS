An unfortunate incidence of fire due to non-availability of MCB at supply take off point at SBC had occurred recently which is to be avoided in future. From the above, it is amply clear that MCB has to be provided at the point of power tapping from SBC panel for protection of 110V DC wiring for FSDS system and that Supplier is bound to carryout the work as per specification irrespective of an approval by purchaser.

In this regard, vide letter under reference, instructions were issued for compliance of the above. Suppliers were also instructed regarding the same during meeting at Carriage Dte, RDSO on dated 18.10.24. However, it has been reported by Zonal Railways that suppliers are showing resistance for the compliance of the above. These observations highlight deviations from the specifications and guidelines issued by RDSO.

Accordingly, it is hereby re-advised that compliance of already issued specifications and guidelines to be ensured without fail at the earliest. Any non-compliance is likely to result in punitive action.
