### Component Designations and Descriptions

| Component | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Trailer Bogie** | Attached with Trailer Coach. Does not have provision of suspended Traction Motor & Gear Box. Non-powered bogie. |
| **Motor Bogie** | Attached with Motor Coach. Traction Motors are suspended from bogies & gear boxes are mounted on the axle. Powered bogie. |
