### Maintenance Manual of VBE Trainset (V2.0)
**Section:** 5. Bogie

#### 5.1.1 FEATURES:
*   **Bolster less bogie:** Carboy rests on air spring – less components.
*   **Speed suitability:** 160kmph.
*   **Max. Axle load:** 17 t.
*   **CTRB:** (Cartridge Tapered Roller Bearing)
*   **Primary suspension:** Helical springs & damper.
*   **Secondary suspension:** Air springs.
*   **Dampers (Secondary):** Vertical, lateral & yaw.
*   **Stabilisers:** Anti roll bar.
*   **Braking:** Wheel mounted disc brakes.
*   **Bogie frame:** Common (basic) for both MC (Motor Coach) & TC (Trailer Coach).
*   **Traction motors:** Fully suspended – less unsprung mass.

#### Additional features compared with existing T-18 bogie:
*   **TPWS (Train Protection & Warning System) / TCAS (Train Collision Avoidance System):** Provision on DTC (Driving Trailer Coach) bogie.
*   **APC receiver:** Provision on DTC bogie.
