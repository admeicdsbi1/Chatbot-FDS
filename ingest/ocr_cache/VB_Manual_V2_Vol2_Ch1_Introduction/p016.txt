### Component Designations and Descriptions
*   **1.2.5.9. SLIDING DOORS (TRANSIT DOORS):** Interior sliding doors that separate the boarding area from the seating area.
*   **1.2.5.10. GANGWAYS:** The flexible part of the train allowing relative movement between coaches and providing a secure/comfortable passageway.

### Technical Specifications and Operational Details
*   **Sliding Door Operation:**
    *   Controlled by radar signal (upon approach) or by pressing a button on the door.
    *   **Power Failure Mode:** Freely movable manually.
    *   **Emergency Mode:** Door is disconnected from power supply when the emergency button is pressed; becomes freely movable manually.
