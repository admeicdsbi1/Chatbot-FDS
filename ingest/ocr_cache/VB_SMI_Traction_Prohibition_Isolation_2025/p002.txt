Isolation procedure for Traction Prohibition

1)Isolation procedure for EP Brake Stuck detection:

➢ In case of stuck brake , TCMS will declare the following Faults
EP Brake stuck detected in Coach
EP Brake stuck detected in Rake
EP Brake stuck detected in Rake Through BC pressure switch .

➢ Traction will be Prohibited by the TCMS and brake will be applied by TCMS till the train speed reaches zero kmph , along with this Pneumatic Brake Legend will be updated in display as

➢ In Driver desk “MIN 1 BRK APPLIED” Lamp will be glow.

➢ Select the “Stuck brake” at Main Screen Bottom Right .

(or)
➢ To get the help screen go to “LEGENDS-->HELP SCREEN” and select the “Stuck brake”
