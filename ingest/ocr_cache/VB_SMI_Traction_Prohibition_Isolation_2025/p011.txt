switch input status(Channel 25) in DIO screen of MCC and
PCC_DTC coaches.
3. Identify the coaches in which the BP pressure switch status is low. Check
for any leakages in BP line, in that coach.
4. If the BP is leaking, Isolate the BP for the coach through isolation switch
and release the EP brake using isolation cocks
5. Then go to BP override settings screen and do the BP override in that
particular coach.
6. If no leakage is found, then directly Go to BP override settings screen and
do the BP override in that particular coach.
