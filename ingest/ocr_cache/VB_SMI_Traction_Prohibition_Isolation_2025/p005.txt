Stuck Brake Detected through Min1 brake TL
****************************************************************************************************
************************************FAULT REASON****************************************************
****************************************************************************************************
EP stuck brake through TL, will be detected because of the following
conditions
1. Train speed is greater than 10 kmph.
2. MCH is in Coast or Motoring.
3. Min 1 brake applied TL feedback is high.
****************************************************************************************************
************************************ISOLATION PROCEDURE*********************************************
****************************************************************************************************
1. Go to brake screen, release the holding brake(Ensure parking brake is
applied in this state for safety)
2. In case After releasing the holding brake, if all pressure is becoming 0.
then go to Min1 brake applied loop screen.
