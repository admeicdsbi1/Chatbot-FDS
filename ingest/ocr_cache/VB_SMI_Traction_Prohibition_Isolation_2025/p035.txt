16) Isolation Procedure for Low priority loop failure:

➢ The System will declare the Fault “ Cab Occupy Regular circuit failed ”

➢ Traction will be Prohibited by the TCMS ,
CAB Occupy Legend will be updated in display as
[Cab]

➢ In Display unit “ No Cab Occ ” at Main Screen Bottom right
[Acknowledge]
[No Cab Occ]

➢ Keep the CAB SELECTOR Switch in HPT mode at
CRW Panel.
[LPT] [HPT]
[CAB SELECTOR]

➢ Then High Priority mode activated , Legend will be
updated in display as
[H Cab]

➢ Then TCMS system will allow the Traction

17) Isolation Procedure for TSL loop1 failure:

➢ Traction will be Prohibited by the TCMS and brake
will be applied by TCMS till the train speed reaches
zero kmph , Legend will be updated in display as
[TSL1]

➢ Select “ TRACTION SELECTION” Switch to
“LOOP2” at CRW panel .
[LOOP1] [LOOP2]
[TRACTION SELECTION]

➢ Then TCMS system will allow the Traction .
