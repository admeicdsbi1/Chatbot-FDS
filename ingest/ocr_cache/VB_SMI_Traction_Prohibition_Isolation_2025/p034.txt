**Emergency OFF loop triggered**
****************************************************************************************************
************************************FAULT REASON****************************************************
****************************************************************************************************
Following conditions may lead to EOL triggering.
1. EOL switch operated by Motor man/Guard/From other cab.
2. Any coach EOL MCB tripping might have happened.
****************************************************************************************************
************************************ISOLATION PROCEDURE*********************************************
****************************************************************************************************
If the EOL triggering is not an intentional action, Then check the MCBs of EOL
loop in TC coach.

➢ If the Emergency off switch is operated in any one of the cabs,
normalize the Emergency off switch to recover the EOL loop
➢ If the EOL triggering is not an intentional action, then check the
MCB s of EOL loop in TC coach.
