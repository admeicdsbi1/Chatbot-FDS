20) Procedure for Train operation while F1 coupler disconnected during running

Train operation in RDM Mode will not work in case of F1 coupler removed.

➢ Train should be operated in Normal (Key ON) mode only

➢ High priority mode should be selected from occupied CAB .

➢ TSL-2 should be selected from occupied CAB .

➢ Door operation can be done by operating the Door switch from other cab .

➢ Brake function will work, since By default 100% command will be considered for brake operation .

➢ Min 1 VCB on and Min 1 Panto up Lamps will not glow in driver desk .

➢ EBL Bypass function will not work .

➢ MIN1 lamp will not glow in driver desk .

➢ Signal bell function will not work .

➢ PIS reset operation will not work .
