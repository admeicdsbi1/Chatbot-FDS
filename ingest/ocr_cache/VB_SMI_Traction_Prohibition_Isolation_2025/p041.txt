21) Procedure for Train operation while F2 coupler disconnected during running

Train operation in RDM Mode will not work in case of F2 coupler removed

➢ Train should be operated in Normal (Key ON) mode only

➢ Keep ADCR bypass switch in Bypass mode in Occupied cab .

➢ PAS function will not work .

➢ Shunting mode will not work .

➢ If Lights functionality issue , Lights can be operated individually self coaches .

➢ Min1 fire detected LED in driver desk will not glow & FDS Buzzer and hooter will not work as it us on the trail line
