19) Procedure for Shunting Operation through Redundant master controller while Main MCH failure:

* Traction will be Prohibited by the TCMS in case of Main Master controller failure
* CAB should be de-occupy from main Master controller

* Keep the Mode Selector switch in Shunting mode in shunting operation panel at Driver desk, Legend will be updated in display as [Sh Cab]
* Keep the Direction selection switch in Forward/Reverse as per direction required
* TE/BE Switch available for generating the Tractive (or) braking effort .
* For TRACTION : Keep TE/BE Switch in 33% initially and press the Drive Push button for traction , after based on the requirement select 67% & 100% along with the drive push button for increasing tractive effort .
* For BRAKING : Keep TE/BE Switch in 33% initially and press the BRAKE Push button for Braking , after based on the requirement select 67% & 100% along with the Brake push button for increasing Braking effort .

* Speed limit push button Use for shunting mode operation only , while pressing this Speed limit push button speed will be limited to 15 KMPH.

**Panel Component Labels:**

* NORMAL - SHUNTING (Mode Selector switch position)
* 33% - 67% - 100% (TE/BE switch positions)
* FWD - REV (Direction selection switch positions)
* SPEED LIMIT (Yellow button)
* BRAKE (Red button)
* DRIVE (Green button)
* SHUNTING OPERATION (Panel title)
