NOTE:
1. UPPER TRIM PANEL SHALL BE FIXED INDIVIDUALLY, WITHOUT DEPENDING ON ADJACENT PANELS TO ATTEND THE MAINTENANCE WORK OF ROLLER BLIND AND DESTINATION BOARD.
2. ALTERNATIVELY ALUMINIUM TO SPEC. IS:737-2008 Gr.40800H2/19000H2 IS ACCEPTABLE.
3. UPPER TRIM PANEL SHALL BE SCREWED AT BOTH ENDS. THE SCREWING HOLES SHALL BE CLOSED WITH PLUG (ITEM-12).
4. PLUG (ITEM-12) SHALL BE SUITABLY DESIGNED TO BLEND WITH OVERALL AESTHETICS AND TO LOCK WITH ALUMINIUM PANELS. IT SHOULD NOT BE EASILY REMOVABLE WITH BARE HANDS AND TO MATCH PANEL COLOUR.
5. BOTH EDGES OF EACH UPPER TRIM PANEL SHALL BE DESIGNED, SUCH THAT IT LOOKS AESTHETICALLY GOOD AND INDEPENDENT.
6. THE JOINT OF UPPER TRIM PANELS SHALL MATCH WITH CENTRE OF INFILL PANELS.
7. COLOUR SHADE OF THE UPPER TRIM PANEL SHALL BE RAL 080 70 10 WITH SMOOTH GEL COACTED FINISH WITH ANTI-GRAFFITTI.
8. BRACKET (ITEM-9) SHALL BE FIXED WITH STRUCTURAL ADHESIVE AT SPECIFIED LOCATION AS SHOWN AND THROUGH HOLES SHALL BE PROVIDED ON UPPER TRIM PANEL ALIGNED TO PANEL SURFACE AS SHOWN.
9. SUITABLE BACK PIECES SHALL BE WELDED TO THE SHELL FOR FIXING THE BRACKETS (ITEM-9) AT SPECIFIED LOCATIONS FOR FIXING THE UPPER TRIM PANEL ALIGNED TO PANEL SURFACE AS SHOWN.
