Sub: Failure of primary Coil Springs in Vande Bharat Trains.

24 failure cases of primary coil springs in Vande Bharat coaches have been reported to RDSO. In most of failures of primary springs, cracks have appeared in 1st active coil i.e. at approx. 1.1-1.4 turn from end. In one case of coil spring failure, which has been reported by SCR in coach no.235012 TC of train no.20833/34 VSKP-SC on 07.01.2024, it was observed that failure/crack appeared in ineffective coil at bottom end i.e. at 0.5 turn from the end.

Accordingly, RDSO official has visited VSKP depot on 12.01.2024 & conducted thorough investigation of the case. In investigation, it has been observed that bottom ineffective end of the failed coil spring was seating on projected weld leg of collar of bottom centering disc (lower spring pad with bump stop). For appreciation, photographs in Annexure-I may be pursued. Point of interaction of projected weld leg with bottom end of coil spring became stress concentration point, resulting in coil spring failure.

It is pertinent that RDSO, vide letter MC/TS/BD date 10.11.2023, has already advised M/s Medha to switchover to single piece design of bottom centering disc (lower spring pad with bump stop).

In view of above, it is requested that thorough examination of bottom centering disc & top spring pad along-with damper & axle box pivot bush should also be done in case of any coil springs failure.

It is also requested that all bottom centering discs (lower spring pad with bump stop) of Vande Bharat trains should be thoroughly inspected at first given opportunity at depot or workshops for identifying any manufacturing defect & further necessary action.
