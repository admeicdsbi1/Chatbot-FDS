Sub: Implementation of modification works related to FSDS system.
Ref: This office letter of even no. dated 27.11.2024 & 13.01.2025.

Vide RDSO letters under reference, all the suppliers have already been advised to carry out the modification works of FSDS system as per guidelines/specifications issued by RDSO as detailed below:

(i) The control supply to the Fire Detection System will be provided from Terminal Block (TB) X1 of Switch Board Cabinet (SBC) for LHB AC coaches and TB-4 for ICF AC coaches through DC MCB of 2 Amp rating along with limiter module.

(ii) Positive and negative wire of 110 V DC supply shall be segregated by placing them in separate conduits as specified in specification.

(iii) Provision of direct power supply from SBC panel for FSDS system.

In regard to above safety related modifications, it was mentioned that all FSDS system installed with specification no. RDSO/2008/CG-04(Rev-05) and onwards, supplier is responsible for implementation of above requirements of specification on Free of Cost (FOC) basis in existing coaches if these clauses of specification have not been complied with.

However, Zonal Railways have enquired about methodology for implementation of above for various revision of specification. So, it is once again reiterated that following methodology to be adopted for implementation of above safety related guidelines as per specification no. RDSO/2008/CG-04:

1. For FSDS system fitted with Rev-05 or later version of specification no. RDSO/2008/CG-04, supplier is responsible for implementation of above safety related modification work on Free of Cost basis if these have not been complied during commissioning as per specification.

2. For coaches fitted with FSDS system of Rev-04 or earlier version of specification no. RDSO/2008/CG-04, above safety related modification work to be carried out on payment basis as the above requirements were incorporated from Rev-05 onwards.
