**Component Designations**
*   **WAGO 770-223 (socket):** Electrical connector (socket)
*   **WAGO 770-233 (plug):** Electrical connector (plug)
*   **Button (1):** Control mechanism for seat reclining
*   **Backrest (2):** Adjustable seat component

**Technical Values**
*   **WAGO 770-223:** Part number for socket connector
*   **WAGO 770-233:** Part number for plug connector

**Operational Procedures**
*   **Reclining the seat:**
    *   Push button (1) and recline the backrest to the desired position (2).
    *   To decline the backrest, push button (1) without applying pressure on the backrest. The backrest will return to neutral position automatically.
