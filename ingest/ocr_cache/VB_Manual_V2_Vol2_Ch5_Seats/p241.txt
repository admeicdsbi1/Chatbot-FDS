### Technical Values
*   **Screw (Front):** M6x20 10.9 A2J ISO 7380
*   **Screw (Back):** M6x60 10.9 A2J ISO 7380
*   **Bolt (Gas spring fixation):** M6x20 8.8 DIN 912
*   **Bolt (Rear fixation):** M8x25 8.8 DIN 7984

### Component Designations
*   **Bush 310008:** Gas spring fixation bush
*   **Rearbush - 310003:** Rear fixation bush
*   **Front bush - 310007:** Front fixation bush
*   **Bolt 210038:** Gas spring assembly bolt

### Maintenance Procedures

**E. Exchange of the gas spring**
1.  Remove cushion according to section 8.3.
2.  Remove the bolt M6x20 8.8 DIN 912 and gas spring fixation bush 310008.
3.  Remove the gas spring lever.
4.  Remove bolt M8x25 8.8 DIN 7984 and rear fixation bush 310003.
5.  Remove gas spring assembly bolt 210038 and front fixation bush 310007.
6.  Replace gas spring.
