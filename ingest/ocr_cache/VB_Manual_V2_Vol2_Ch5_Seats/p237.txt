### Technical Index: Seat and Footrest Mechanism

**Subject:** Seat Rotation and Footrest Adjustment Procedures

**Component Procedures:**

*   **Seat Rotation Mechanism:**
    *   **Initial Rotation:** Start rotating the seat (around 15 degrees) by pushing or pulling the handle.
    *   **CAUTION:** The rotation is possible only in one direction. If the seat blocks when rotating, then try the other direction.
    *   **Locking:** Rotate the seat to 180 degrees until the mechanism locks itself.

*   **Footrest (Section E):**
    *   **Release:** Lift the footrest.
    *   **Adjustment:** Lift to the desired position.
    *   **Locking Positions:** Three lock positions are available.

**Technical Values:**
*   **Rotation Angle (Initial):** 15 degrees
*   **Rotation Angle (Locked):** 180 degrees
*   **Footrest Lock Positions:** 3

**Document References:**
*   **Section Heading:** E. Footrest
*   **Section Heading:** Chair-car class seat
