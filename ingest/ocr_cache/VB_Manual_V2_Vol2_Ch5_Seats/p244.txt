**Technical Values & Specifications:**
*   **Bolt (Armrest):** M6x16 8.8 A2J ISO 4762
*   **Torque Specification:** 11 Nm
*   **Bolt (Gas spring lever):** M6x20 8.8 DIN 912

**Component Designations:**
*   **Top arm:** Upper section of the armrest assembly.
*   **Bottomarm:** Lower section of the armrest assembly.
*   **Gas spring lever:** Lever mechanism for seat adjustment.
*   **Sleeve (bush):** Component used in conjunction with the M6x20 bolt for the lever assembly.
*   **Armrest panel:** Replaceable panel component of the armrest module.

**Maintenance Procedures:**
*   **Armrest Replacement:**
    1. Remove cushion (ref: section 8.3).
    2. Decline seat backrest to neutral position.
    3. Remove four M6x16 8.8 A2J ISO 4762 bolts.
    4. Remove armrest.
    5. Install new armrest (verify part number per section 8.8.1).
    6. Apply medium thread locker to bolt threads.
    7. Tighten four M6x16 8.8 A2J ISO 4762 bolts to 11 Nm.
*   **Middle Armrest Module Exchange:**
    1. Remove gas spring lever (remove M6x20 8.8 DIN 912 bolt and sleeve).
    2. Replace armrest panel (verify part number).
