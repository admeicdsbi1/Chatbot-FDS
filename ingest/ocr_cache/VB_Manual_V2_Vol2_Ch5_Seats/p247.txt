### Maintenance Procedures

**Tray Lock:**
1. Remove the 3 screws screw 4.2x19 Inox DIN 7982
2. Remove old lock
3. Assemble the lock together: 310998 (rotating lock), 310999 (lock body), 214746 (spring), 311023 (washer) and 410011 (screw)
4. Position the lock onto the back of the seat
5. Tighten the 3 screws 4.2x19 Inox DIN 7982

**Exchange of the footrest (Executive Class):**
1. Remove bolts M8x25 DIN 7984
2. Exchange footrest
3. Add medium thread locker on tip of screws
4. Tighten the 4 screws M8x25 DIN 7984 with washer DIN 125 and nut M8 DIN 985
