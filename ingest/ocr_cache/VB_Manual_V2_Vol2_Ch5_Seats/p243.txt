### Component Designations
*   **Bush 310008:** Bushing component for gas spring lever assembly.
*   **M6x20:** Bolt for gas spring lever.
*   **Lever:** Gas spring activation lever.
*   **Top arm:** Upper section of the armrest assembly.
*   **Bottomarm:** Lower section of the armrest assembly.
*   **M6x16 ISO 4762:** Fastening bolts for armrest modules.

---

### Maintenance Procedures Summary

**G. Exchange of Side armrest module with button**
1.  Remove gas spring lever (Remove bolt M6x20 8.8 DIN 912 and sleeve).
2.  Replace armrest panel.
3.  Verify part number.
4.  Position lever: long arm in contact with roll under button; short arm inside armrest.
5.  Install bush and tighten bolt M6x20 8.8 DIN 912.

**H. Exchange of Middle armrest module without button**
1.  Decline seat backrest to neutral position.
2.  Remove bolts M6x16 8.8 A2J ISO 4762.
3.  Replace armrest panel.
4.  Verify part number (refer to section 8.8.1).
5.  Apply medium thread locker to bolt threads.
6.  Tighten five bolts (M6x16 8.8 A2J ISO 4762) to 11 Nm.
