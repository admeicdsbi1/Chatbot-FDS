### Technical Index: VBE Trainset Maintenance

*   **Title:** Maintenance Manual of VBE Trainset (V2.0)
*   **Section:** 5. Maintenance - Seats
*   **Document ID:** IRCAMTECH/GWL/2022-23/T-18/MM/2.0

**Component Designations & Specifications:**
*   **WAGO 770-223:** Socket (electricity connector)
*   **WAGO 770-233:** Plug (electricity connector)
*   **M5x10:** Screw size for trap door
*   **M10:** Screw size for leg and wall fixation

**Technical Values & Dimensions:**
*   **Screw quantity:** 4 (for trap door)
*   **Hole diameter (side panel):** 11 mm
*   **Hole diameter (wall connector):** 10 mm
*   **Dimension (width):** 260 ± 1 mm
*   **Dimension (vertical spacing):** 150 ± 1 mm

**Maintenance Instructions:**
*   **Electrical Connection:** Connect electricity connector (one from rotation mechanism, one from ground) using WAGO 770-223 (socket) and 770-233 (plug).
*   **Trap Door:** Close trap door and tighten 4 x M5x10 screws; apply medium strength thread locker to the tip of each screw.
*   **Seat Assembly:** Position the leg and the wall connector; tighten fixation screws (leg and wall) with M10 screws.

**Safety Cautions:**
*   **CAUTION:** Screw type, torque, and use of medium strength thread locker for the fixation of the carrier to the tram structure must be defined by the vehicle manufacturer.
