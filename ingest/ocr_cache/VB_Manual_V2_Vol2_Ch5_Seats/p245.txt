### Maintenance Procedures Summary

**J. Exchange of the central console armrest pad**
1. Open the armrest on the central console.
2. Remove the 2 screws M6x12 10,9 DIN7991.
3. Exchange the insert.
4. Add medium thread locker on tip of screws.
5. Tighten the screws M6x12.

**K. Exchange of table (Chair Car)**
1. Remove backrest.
2. Table is attached on the upper fixation holes of the seat frame.
