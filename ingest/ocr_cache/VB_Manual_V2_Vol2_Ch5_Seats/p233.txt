**Document Information**

*   **Reference Number:** IRCAMTECH/GWL/2022-23/T-18/MM/2.0

**Technical Values & Specifications**
*   **Mounting Screw Size:** M10
*   **Trap Door Screw Specification:** 4 x M5x10 A2 70- ISO 7380-2
*   **Mounting Hole Diameter:** $\phi$ 10.5
*   **Mounting Plate Dimensions:** 450 $\pm$ 1 mm (length) x 220 $\pm$ 1 mm (width)
*   **Connector Reference:** WAGO WITH COVER 770 223 / 770 233
*   **Dimension Reference (Front):** 125 mm
*   **Dimension Reference (Intermediate):** 167.7 mm
*   **Dimension Reference (End):** 17.5 mm
*   **Dimension Reference (Vertical):** 45.7 mm / 28 mm

**Component Designations**
*   **Connector WAGO:** Electrical connection point located in the centre of the rotation mechanism.
*   **Trap door:** Access panel on the rotation mechanism secured by M5x10 screws.
*   **Rotation mechanism:** Base assembly for the executive class seat.

**Maintenance Instructions (Summary)**
1.  Position the seat carrier onto the vehicle floor with the electric connection in the centre of the rotation mechanism.
2.  Fasten the four mounting screws (M10).
3.  Open the trap door on the rotation mechanism (using 4 x M5x10 A2 70- ISO 7380-2 screws) and remove the trap door.

**Safety Caution**
*   **Note:** Screw type, torque, and use of medium strength thread locker for the fixation of the seat to the train structure must be defined by the train manufacturer.
